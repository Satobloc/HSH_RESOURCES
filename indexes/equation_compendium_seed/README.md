**Snapshot:** 27 November 2026  
**Coverage:** first compilation tranche; not an exhaustive archive harvest.

- `SAT_EQUATION_COMPENDIUM_SEED.json.gz` — gzip-compressed UTF-8 JSON containing 69 equation occurrences, 15 source documents, six sector-document mappings, source paths and blob identities, source equations, assumptions, provenance/maturity fields, and a document index.
- `NATHAN_DIRECT_PROJECTION_ANGLE_SEED.json.gz` — gzip-compressed UTF-8 JSON containing 10 direct user-role messages from two archived conversations. This is a direct-role extract only; implicit voice/context attribution and the wider sector-by-sector commentary harvest remain incomplete.

Decompress with `gzip -dk filename.json.gz` and parse the resulting UTF-8 JSON. The public-facing sector PDFs are published separately in `Satobloc/HsH/PUBLIC_SITE/PAPERS/`; their original source citations and links are embedded in the PDFs.
