# H(s)H / SAT Prior-Art Log

Purpose: compact, claim-level comparison of H(s)H/SAT constructions against identified earlier work. This is a **prior-art triage log**, not a novelty determination. “Not identified” means only that the feature was not found in the sources reviewed for that entry.

Status terms: **Strongly anticipated** = substantially present in earlier work; **Partly anticipated** = a material substructure or relation is present; **Standard mathematics** = ordinary established mathematical machinery rather than a distinctive construction; **Not identified in reviewed set** = not found in the cited sources reviewed so far.

## Logging rule

Add rows at the **smallest useful claim/component level** rather than one verdict per paper. A single source may strongly anticipate one component while leaving another untouched. Separate (1) established mathematics, (2) specific earlier constructions, (3) H(s)H/SAT packaging or solver usage, and (4) physical interpretation. Do not infer novelty from absence in this log; record only what the reviewed evidence supports.
