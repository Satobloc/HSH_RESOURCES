EVERY CUSP SINGULARITY LINK ADMITS INFINITELY
MANY STRONG SYMPLECTIC FILLINGS
NAOHIKO KASUYA AND TAKAHIRO OBA
ABSTRACT. In this paper, we show that if the link of an isolated com-
plex surface singularity is either aSol3-manifold or an fSL(2;R)-manifold
with its canonical contact structure, then it admits infinitely many strong
symplectic fillings that are pairwise non-diffeomorphic and not related
by a sequence of blow-ups or blow-downs. As a consequence, the link
of any cusp singularity, exceptional unimodal singularity, or hyperbolic
Brieskorn singularity admits infinitely many pairwise non-diffeomorphic
minimal strong symplectic fillings.
1.INTRODUCTION
One of the principal sources of contact3-manifolds is the links of iso-
lated hypersurface singularities in three complex variables. These singu-
larities have associated Milnor fibers and minimal resolutions, which pro-
vide canonicalstrong symplectic fillingsof the links. Here, by a strong sym-
plectic filling of a closed positive contact3-manifold(M,ξ), we mean a
compact connected symplectic4-manifold(X,ω)with boundary such that
∂X=Mas oriented manifolds and there exists a Liouville vector field
Vdefined near the boundary pointing outward along∂Xwhich satisfies
ξ= ker(ι Vω)|T∂X .
Based on the modalitym, which measures the complexity of singulari-
ties, Arnol’d classified singularities up to modality2; see [1]. Singularities
withm= 0,1are calledsimpleandunimodal, respectively. Unimodal singu-
larities fall into three types:simple elliptic( ˜E6, ˜E7, ˜E8),cusp(T pqr), and the
14exceptional singularities (E 12,E 13,E 14,Z 11,Z 12,Z 13,Q 10,Q 11,Q 12,W 12,
W13,S 11,S 12,U 12). The geometry of the links of singularities withm≤1is
well-studied. Neumann [23] showed that the links of simple, simple ellip-
tic, cusp, and the14exceptional singularities are diffeomorphic to quotients
of simply connected Lie groupsSU(2),Nil 3,Sol 3, and fSL(2;R), respec-
tively, by cocompact lattices. Moreover, the canonical contact structures on
these links agree with those induced by left-invariant contact structures on
these simply connected Lie groups [6].

following question.

singularities, is there a singularity whose link admits infinitely many Stein fillings
up to homotopy equivalent?
