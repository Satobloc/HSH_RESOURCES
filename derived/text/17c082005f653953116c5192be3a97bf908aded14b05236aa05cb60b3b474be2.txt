Mutation matrices from Poisson CGL extensions
Zihang Liu and Jiang-Hua Lu and Yipeng Mi
Abstract
Symmetric Poisson CGL extensions form a particular class of polynomial Poisson algebras
that are shown by K. Goodearl and M. Yakimov to admit compatible cluster structures. In
this paper, we give explicit formulas for a family of mutation matrices in the Goodearl-
Yakimov theory via matrix products as well as by entry-wise description.

1 Introduction and statements of main results
1.1 Introduction
Among the systematical examples of commutative and associative algebras admitting (meaning-
ful) cluster structures [FZ02] are a class of polynomial Poisson algebras studied by K. Goodearl
and M. Yakimov in [GY14, GY23].
Letkbe a field of characteristic 0. A lengthniterated Poisson-Ore extension (ofk) is a
polynomial Poisson algebra (k[x 1,...,x n],{,}) such that
{k[x1,...,x j−1], xj}⊂x jk[x1,...,x j−1] +k[x 1,...,x j−1],∀2≤j≤n.(1.1)
Such an iterated Poisson-Ore extension is said to besymmetricif it also satisfies
{xj,k[x j+1,...,x n]}⊂x jk[xj+1,...,x n] +k[xj+1,...,x n],∀1≤j≤n−1.(1.2)
Given a splitk-torusT, aT-Poisson CGL extension(reps.symmetricT-Poisson CGL exten-
sion), as defined in [GY14, GY23] and named after G. Cauchon, K. Goodearl, and E. Letzter,
is an iterated Poisson-Ore extension (resp. symmetric iterated Poisson-Ore extension) together
with a compatibleT-action and certain nilpotency condition. See§1.2 for details.
In a remarkable theory developed in [GY14, GY23], K. Goodearl and M. Yakimov showed
that a presentation of a Poisson algebra (R,{,}) as asymmetricT-Poisson CGL extension gives

In the theory of cluster algebras, it is often desirable to have a good and easily workable
description of the mutation matrices, as each one of them completely determines the cluster
structure up to isomorphisms. For asymmetricT-Poisson CGL extension (R,{,}) of lengthn,
Goodearl and Yakimov constructed in [GY23] (again under some mild conditions on scalars and
Poisson CGL generators) a family of seeds (y τ,Mτ) in Σ GY, whereτlies in a certain subset Ξ n
of the symmetric groupS n. The mutation matrixM τ for eachτ∈Ξ n is characterized in [GY23,
Theorem 11.1] as the unique solution to a certain system of linear equations, which we refer to
asGSV Equations(after M. Gekhtman, M. Shapiro, and A. Vainstein), that are defined by the
Poisson coefficient matrix ofy τ and theT-characters of the elements iny τ. The existence and
uniqueness ofM τ are proved in [GY23, Theorem 11.1] by a rather involved induction procedure.
In this paper, for any lengthnsymmetric Poisson CGL extensionRand for eachτ∈Ξ n, we
solve the GSV Equations onM τ explicitly, and we do so by first proving some general results
on Poisson CGL extensions that are not necessarily symmetric.
More precisely, for an arbitraryT-Poisson CGL extension (R,{,}) of lengthn,not neces-
sarily symmetric, we use the sequencey= (y 1,...,y n) ofhomogeneous Poisson prime elements
ofRdefined in [GY23] to formulate the GSV Equations on a matrixM(see (1.9)). Then
1) by some elementary linear algebra arguments, we show that a solutionMto the GSV
Equations, if exists, must be given by a certain explicit matrix product, implying in particular
the uniqueness of such anM;
2) by a computation on the Poisson bracket{,}, we describe an explicit integer solutionM
to the GSV Equations via the expansion of certain elements ofRas Laurent polynomials ofy;
3) under some mild conditions on scalars (to ensure thatMis skew-symmetrizable) and
normality ofR(Definition 3.5.1), we show that the upper cluster algebra defined by (y,M)
(with no frozen variables inverted) coincides with the polynomial ringR.
When theT-Poisson CGLRis symmetric, it is shown in [GY23] that the same Poisson
algebra (R,{,}) becomes a (in general no longer symmetric)T-Poisson CGL extensionR τ in
the coordinates (xτ(1),...,x τ(n)) for eachτ∈Ξ n. Applying our aforementioned results on (the
not necessarily symmetric)T-Poisson CGL extensionR τ, we arrive at not only the existence
and uniqueness but also explicit formulas ofM τ as matrix products.
A classification of symmetricT-Poisson CGL extensions is recently given in [ML25] in terms
of their log-canonical terms and the secondT-invariant Poisson cohomology of the log-canonical
terms. In particular, it is shown in [ML25] that there is a collection of non-positive integers,
calledCartan integers, associated to the log-canonical term of any symmetricT-Poisson CGL
extensionR. As an application of our explicit formulas, we show that the non-zero entries of
Mτ, for everyτ∈Ξ n, are either±1 or±a, whereais a negative Cartan integer associated to
the log-canonical term ofR.
