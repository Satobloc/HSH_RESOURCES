I. THE SECRET IDENTITY OF THE CONFORMAL ALGEBRA 
Recall the conformal algebra1 for Rp,q is given by translations Pµ, rotations Lµν , special conformal transformations 
Kµ, and dilatations D, satisfying the algebra 
[D, Pµ] = iPµ [D, Kµ] = −iKµ [Kµ, Pν ] = 2i(ηµν D − Lµν ) (1) 
[Kρ, Lµν ] = i(ηρµKν − ηρν Kµ) [Pρ, Lµν ] = i(ηρµPν − ηρν Pµ) (2) 
[Lµν , Lρσ ] = i(ηνρLµσ + ηµσ Lνρ − ηµρLνσ − ηνσ Lµρ) (3) 
Of this algebra really only the ﬁrst line should be unfamiliar, as the second line simply says that Pµ and Kµ are 
vectors and the last line is just the Lorentz algebra SO(p, q). Now to put this into a more intuitive form we imagine

1...p + q). Now consider the algebra deﬁned by the following antisymmetric tensor generators Jab (where a, b include

depends on the connectedness of the Feynman diagram being considered.
