ABSTRACT: This research proposal presents the complete framework for the "Lunar Structural
Code" (LSC), a unified deterministic engineering design standard tailored for construction in extreme extraterr
estrial environments. A ddressing structural mechanics under low-gravity (1/6th Earth g),
ultra-high vacuum, extreme thermal cycling (Δ T ≈ 250 K), space radiation, and micrometeorite risks,
the LSC establishes unified safety factors, geotechnical evaluation rules for subterranean basaltic
lava tubes, performance classes for 3D-printed lunar regolith concrete, hybrid composite pressure-
vessel mechanics, thermal expansion allowances, and non-destructive testing (NDT) quality
verification protocols.
1 . INTRODUCTION & SCOPE
 
        
      
1.1 Purpose: To define a standardized Limit State Design (LSD) methodology for load-
bearing surface structures and subterranean lava tube habitats on the Moon.
1.2 Scope of Application: Pressurized crew habitats, unpressurized protective shields,
surface landing pads, vertical thermal barriers, regolith retainment walls, and subterranean rock anc
horage systems.
