Relativistic Lagrangian mechanics
In theoretical physics, relativistic Lagrangian mechanics is Lagrangian mechanics applied in the
context of special relativity and general relativity.

similar manner but without including rest energy. The form of the Lagrangian also makes the
relativistic action functional proportional to the proper time of the path in spacetime.
