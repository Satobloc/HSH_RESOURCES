Abstract12
In the paper it is demonstrated that, given Alice and Bob hold equal copies
of measuring instruments, the claim that theoretically the quantum corre-
lation can be written as a Bell correlation is refuted. The hypothesis that
theoretically the quantum correlation in entangled polarized photons is mis-
construed.
Keywords:Entangled photons, Bell’s correlation formula, equal13
measurement functions, misconstruction of hypotheses14
1. Introduction15
It all starts with Einstein not agreeing with the completeness of quan-16
tum mechanics. That happened already early in the first stages of the the-17
ory (Howard, 1990). Later Einstein developed a more detailed version of18
his criticism and invented what nowadays is called entanglement (Einstein19
et al., 1935). Later Einstein wrote a less ”Gelehnrter” see a.o. (Howard,20
1990) ,(Butzer et al., 2025), version that better suited his philosophical taste,21
(A.Einstein, 1948). In the later stages of Einstein’s thinking about quantum22
mechanics (Butzer et al., 2025), Einstein in a letter to Lionel Cooper, clearly23
distinguished between inner contradiction and incompleteness.24
The problem with the claim that there must be hidden variables (that25
restore causality and locality) is that no example or direction to search are26
given by Einstein. Einstein leaves it to next generations to figure out a local27
hidden variables theory of quantum entanglement.28
In the fifties, Aharonov and Bohm simplified the notion of entanglement29
to spin entanglement (Bohm and Aharonov, 1957). Later, John Bell em-30
ployed the spin entanglement in his formulation of the spin (polarization)31
entanglement correlation formula (Bell, 1964).32

3. Conclusion & discussion122
We may therefore conclude that it was demonstrated thatH 0 is false.123
ButH 0 is not false on grounds of gathered data. One of the anglesψ a,b, ψa,d124
orψ d,b, two or perhaps all three, are not in accordance withH 0. It is a125
misconstrued hypothesis. This is so because the inequality (5) only refers126
to the formulation of the hypothesisH 0. The failure ofH 0 does not tell127
us anything about possible Einstein affirmative data at all. It is also not a128
famous Bell inequality. It is the demonstration of a misconstrued hypothesis129
because someψ a,b, ψa,d and/orψ d,b already destroy the required generality of130
H0. What is the use of a hypothetical correlation when some combinations131
of unit parameter vectors for sure don’t work?132
The formulatedH 0 can not, from the start of an empirical research, only133
be considered valid forsomesymmetrical combinations of three different unit134
length vectors to be found onS. What do you do with the ones that don’t135
comply toH 0? The conclusion is therefore that it is not possible withH 0 to136
coveralla,banddinS. And that refers back to the impossibility to have137
an all encompassingE(a,b) in (1). And we note that the inequality (5) is138
derived in the same manner as other Bell inequalities.139
Hence, when one usesH 0 andH 1 in an empirical exploration, the mis-140
construction ofH 0 will show itself for equal type of measuring instrument141
functions,A(x,λ) for both Alice and Bob. If an experimental researcher142
wants to contest this ”equal type of measuring instrument functions” point143
with an experiment, it first and foremost must be demonstrated that the144
measurement function of Alice, i.e.Aand the measuring instrument func-145
tion of Bob, i.e.Brespond differently to a given settings given aλ. That146
appears to be impossible because the functionsA(x,λ) =±1 andB(y,λ)147
are unknown. With the same type of measuring instrument for Alice and148
Bob one may, however, initially assume that each measurement instrument149
will respond similarly to input. And note that the quantum correlation has150
a·b=b·a.151
