1. A Question Epistemology Must Answer
Every epistemological system does the same thing: it tells others "what counts as reliable knowledge." It
providesastandard,andthensays:claimsthatsatisfythisstandardarereliable,andthosethatdonotarenot.
But there is a question that all epistemological systems must answer: does the standard you provide
satisfy itself?
If the standard provided by an epistemological system does not satisfy itself—if it cannot be supported or
refuted by any empirical evidence—then how can it tell others "what counts as reliable knowledge"? It teaches
others how to distinguish truth from falsehood, while standing itself outside the adjudication of truth and
falsehood.Thisisnotepistemology;thisisprivilege.
The predicament of traditional epistemology lies in this: its claims can be simultaneously interpreted as
asserting facts about the world and as proposing methodological conventions. Descartes' "I think" can be
interpreted as asserting the existence of the self and its epistemological priority, or as recommending a method
of doubt. Kant's "the thing-in-itself is unknowable" can be interpreted as asserting a universal boundary of
humancognitivecapacity,orasclarifyingtherulesofusefortheconceptof"experience."Popper'sfalsifiability
standard can be interpreted as asserting a necessary condition for scientific knowledge, or as proposing a
demarcation convention. When the authority of the standard needs to be established, they are interpreted as
factual claims; when the legitimacy of the standard itself is questioned, they are interpreted as methodological
conventions.Thisdualinterpretabilityrendersthemneithertestablenortrustworthy.
2. From the First Person to the Third Person
This paper is not proposing a new viewpoint within the framework of traditional epistemology. It is a
thoroughtransformationofthesubjectpositionofepistemology.
Descartes founded epistemology on the first person—"I think." For nearly four centuries thereafter,
epistemologyhasbeenavariationofthefirstperson:Kant'sfirst-personreasonreflectingonitsownconditions,
