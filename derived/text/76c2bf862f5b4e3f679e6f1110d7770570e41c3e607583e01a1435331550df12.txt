Some simple beamformer equations 
Again, at this level, the simple concepts of beamforming should be well known, but at the risk of a little 
triviality (never a great risk….people are good at skipping over material they already know!), here are a 
few small concepts and equations that are useful. They are: 1) the plane wave beamformer, 2) the 
focused beamformer, and 3) the grating lobe equation.
