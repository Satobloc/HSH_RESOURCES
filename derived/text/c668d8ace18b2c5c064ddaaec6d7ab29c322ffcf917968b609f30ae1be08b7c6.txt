NEW CONSTRUCTIONS RELATING REAL AND COMPLEX
CONTACT STRUCTURES
ALI M. ELGINDI
Abstract.We establish new connections between real and complex contact
geometry via embeddings of 3-manifolds intoC 3. We introduce a newcontact
wedgeconstruction combining two transverse real contact structures into a
complex one, subject to obstructions measured by the Nijenhuis tensor and
Dolbeault cohomology. Dually, we form ahedgeconstruction which extracts
real contact structures from complex ones. Applying these tools, we prove that
C3 admits uncountably many complex contact structures, using constructions
over the standard contactS 3 with Legendrian non-isotopic knots as complex
tangents.
MSC: 32V40, 57K33
Key words: contact structure, holomorphic
1.Introduction
We will make a relationship between real and complex contact structures in
the sense that we can construct new complex contact structures using a pair of
transverse real contact structures, in a process we call the ”contact wedge”. This
construction will be subject to the vanishing of two topological invariants.
Theorem 1.Let(ξ 1,ξ 2)be two transverse contact structures on a real 3-manifold
M. Then their wedgeδ=ξ 1∧ξ 2 defines an almost complex structure on a com-
plex 2-plane bundle, which extends to a complex contact structure in a neighbor-
hood ofMprovided the Nijenhuis tensor vanishes and the Dolbeault obstruction in
H 1(M,O(ξ 1⊗C))is trivial.
We will also formulate a new construction which we call the ”hedge” from which
we can construct a real contact structureξover a real 3-manifoldMwhich is itself a
submanifold of a complex manifoldNadmitting a given complex contact structure.

4.The Hedge
The hedge is the dual construction: extracting a real contact structure from a
complex one.
Definition 8(Hedge).Let(N,ψ)be a complex contact manifold of dimension
2n+ 1, and letξ⊂ψbe a holomorphic line subbundle. LetM⊂Nbe a totally real
submanifold of real dimension2n+ 1that is Legendrian with respect toIm(ψ). We
sayξis ahedgeofψoverMifξ| M is a real contact structure onM.
Theorem 9(Hedge Induces Contact Structure).Let(N,ψ)be a complex contact
manifold of dimension 3, andξ⊂ψa holomorphic line subbundle. LetM⊂Nbe
a totally real 3-manifold that is Legendrian with respect toIm(ψ). Thenξ| M is a
real contact structure onM.
Proof.Letθbe a holomorphic 1-form definingψ, soθ∧dθ̸= 0. Sinceξ⊂ψ= kerθ,
the formθvanishes onξ. RestrictθtoM. By the totally real condition,T pM∩
J(TpM) ={0}, so the real and imaginary parts ofθ| M are linearly independent.
Defineα= Re(θ| M). The Legendrian condition ensuresα∧dα̸= 0. Since kerα=
ξ|M by construction,ξ| M is a contact structure.□

5.Abundance of Complex Contact Structures
Theorem 12(Uncountability of Complex Contact Structures).The set of complex
contact structures onC 3 up to isotopy is uncountable.
Proof.We construct an uncountable family{ψ α}α∈I of complex contact structures
onC 3 that are pairwise non-isotopic.
Let (S 3,ξ std) be the standard contact 3-sphere. There exists an uncountable
family of Legendrian knots{L α}α∈I in (S 3,ξ std) that are smoothly isotopic but
Legendrian non-isotopic. For instance, one may take a family of torus knots whose
rotation numbersα∈R/Zare distinct. This is a standard result in Legendrian
knot theory [6].
Choose an overtwisted contact structureξ ot onS 3 that is transverse toξ std. Such
a pair exists because overtwisted structures are flexible and can be chosen to be

transverse to a given tight structure (see [4]). The space of contact structures onS 3
is large, and by the parametrich-principle for contact structures, we may assume
that the pair (ξ std,ξ ot) varies continuously with the parameterαwhen restricted
to appropriate neighborhoods of the knots.
For each Legendrian knotLα, we apply the corresponding explicit embedding as
by our theorem [3] to obtain an embedding
Fα :S 3 ,→C 3
such that the complex tangents ofF α are exactlyL α and the holomorphic tangent
planes alongL α areξ std|Lα.
The pair (ξ std,ξ ot) on the abstractS 3, together with the embeddingF α, defines
aformal complex contact structureδ α alongF α(S3) inC 3. Concretely, the wedge
constructionδ α =ξ std⊕Jξ ot gives an almost complex structure on a complex 2-
plane bundle overFα(S3). considering this bundle together with the almost complex
structure, will constitute a formal solution of the complex contact relation in the
sense of Forstneriˇ c [9]. The this formal construction varies continuously withα
because the embeddingsF α and the contact structures can be chosen continuously.
We now apply Forstneriˇ c’s parametrich-principle as given in Forstneriˇ c [9] which
provided for a parametrich-principle of complex contact structures on Stein man-
ifolds. Specifically, a continuous family of formal complex contact structures along
a closed totally real submanifoldM⊂X(whereXis Stein) can be deformed into a
family of genuine holomorphic complex contact structures on a Stein neighborhood
ofM, keeping the deformation fixed on a prescribed closed subset.
HereX=C 3 (Stein) andM=F α(S3) (totally real except alongL α). Applying
the parametrich-principle to the family{δ α}yields a family{ψ α}of genuine holo-
morphic complex contact structures defined on a Stein neighborhood ofF α(S3) in
C3. BecauseC 3 is Stein and contractible, eachψ α extends uniquely to all ofC 3.
Supposeψ α andψ β are isotopic as complex contact structures forα̸=β. Then
their formal data would be homotopic (by theh-principle), which would imply that
the Legendrian knotsL α andL β are Legendrian isotopic in (S 3,ξ std). However,
by constructionL α andL β have distinct rotation numbers and are therefore not
Legendrian isotopic. This contradiction shows that{ψ α}α∈I is an uncountable
family of pairwise non-isotopic complex contact structures onC 3.□
6.Conclusion
We have introduced two constructions relating real and complex contact struc-
tures. The contact wedge combines two transverse real contact structures into a
complex contact structure on a neighborhood inC 3, subject to vanishing of the
Nijenhuis tensor and a Dolbeault obstruction. The hedge extracts a real contact
structure from a complex one by restriction to a totally real Legendrian submani-
fold. As an application, we showed thatC 3 admits uncountably many non-isotopic
complex contact structures.
