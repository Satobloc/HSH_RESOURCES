Temporal Non-Constructibility in General Relativity:
Lapse, Foliations, and the Operational Limits of Time
Asaad N. Riaz
Riaz Communications Inc.
Correspondence: asaad@quantumhub.solutions
General Relativity describes spacetime through arbitrary foliations by spacelike hypersurfaces,
each labeled by a coordinate timet and associated lapse functionN (x,t ). These quantities enter the
ADM evolution equations, yet the theory contains no distinguished slicing or preferred temporal
rate. This raises a fundamental question: can any internal observer operationally recover the global
time parameter used in a foliation?
We address this by formulating and proving theRelativistic Temporal Non-Constructibility Theorem.
Working strictly within classical Lorentzian geometry and the ADM3 + 1decomposition, we model
an observer as a timelike worldline with access only to events in its causal past. We show that for
any two foliations related by a smooth, strictly monotonic reparametrizationt↦→t′ =f (t), the lapse
transforms asN↦→N′ = N (dt/dt′), while the four-geometry, causal structure, and proper time
along all worldlines remain invariant. As a consequence, every scalar observable accessible to an
internal observer yields the same empirical history in both descriptions. The coordinate timet and
lapse functionN(x,t)are thereforegauge variables with no operational significance.
This result clarifies several structural features of general relativity. It provides the operational
foundation for the absence of global simultaneity, explains why the ADM Hamiltonian is a sum of
constraints withH≈ 0, and shows that the timeless form of the Wheeler-DeWitt equation reflects
temporal gauge freedom rather than a failure of quantization. The theorem generalizes the classical
Temporal Non-Constructibility Theorem for autonomous dynamical systems, establishing that global
time parameters are unobservable in both Newtonian and relativistic settings.
Proper time along worldlines, the geometric invariant determined by the metric, is the only
empirically meaningful temporal quantity in general relativity. Global coordinate time plays no
physical role beyond labeling slices of a foliation. This is the content of temporal non-constructibility
in GR.
I. INTRODUCTION
The description of physical evolution in general relativ-
ity relies on a foliation of spacetime by spacelike hypersur-
faces. In the ADM formulation, [1] a spacetime(M,gab)
is decomposed into slicesΣt labeled by a real parameter
t, and Einstein’s equations are expressed as evolution
equations for geometric data on these slices. The lapse
function N(x,t )determines the amount of proper time
between adjacent hypersurfaces, while the shift vector
Ni(x,t )encodes how spatial coordinates are transported
between them. Although this construction provides a
convenient Hamiltonian representation of the theory, the
parameter t itself is not part of the physical geometry. It
merely labels members of the chosen foliation.
This raises a fundamental question:
Does the coordinate timet used in a foliation have opera-
tional significance for an internal observer, or is it purely
a gauge artifact?
In the standard formulation of general relativity, no
structure intrinsic to the metric singles out any partic-
ular foliation or any particular rate of advance of the
slicing parameter. Different choices of lapse correspond
to different ways of assigning labels to the same family
of hypersurfaces. Because the geometry of spacetime is
invariant under diffeomorphisms, and because observers
are represented as worldlines within that geometry, it is
not clear whether any observer can extract information
about which foliation or which lapse function was used
to generate an ADM decomposition.
A related question arises in classical autonomous
dynamical systems. There, the Temporal Non-
Constructibility Theorem (TNT) [2] demonstrates that
the external time parametert appearing in the equations
of motion is not an observable quantity: reparametriza-
tions of the formt↦→f(t)leave all observationally ac-
cessible histories invariant. The classical theory thereby
distinguishes the geometric orbit traced by the system
from the arbitrary parameter used to describe its traver-
sal.
Thepresentworkestablishesthepreciseanalogueofthis
result in general relativity.We prove that the foliation-
time coordinatet and the lapse functionN(x,t )are op-
erationally non-constructible: no internal observer, mod-
eled as a timelike worldline with access only to events
in its causal past, can measure or infer the global slicing
parameter used in an ADM formulation of the space-
time. Proper time along worldlines is invariant under any
smooth reparametrization of the foliation-time coordinate,
and the observer’s recorded history of scalar observables
is unaffected. Consequently, all physical predictions of
the theory are independent of the choice of lapse.
This result explains, in operational terms, several struc-
tural features of general relativity: the absence of a pre-
ferred notion of global simultaneity, the vanishing of the
canonical Hamiltonian in the ADM formalism, and the
timeless character of the Wheeler-DeWitt equation [3]

II. RELA TIVISTIC FRAMEWORK
This section establishes the geometric and dynamical
structures required for the relativistic formulation of tem-

within the standard setting of classical general relativity,
without introducing additional assumptions or structures
beyond those ordinarily used in the ADM treatment of
gravitational dynamics. All relevant concepts, spacetime,
observers, foliations, and lapse functions, are defined ex-

simple and systematic manner.
A. Spacetime as a Lorentzian Manifold
A spacetime is specified by a smooth, connected,
four-dimensional manifoldM equipped with a smooth
Lorentzian metricgab of signature(−+++). No preferred
coordinates or global time functions are assumed. The ge-

For the purposes of formulating the temporal non-

globally hyperbolic.[4, 5] This ensures that well-posed
initial-value formulations exist: every solution of Ein-

is standard in the ADM formulation and guarantees the
existence of foliations by Cauchy surfaces.
No foliation is regarded as physically distinguished;
each arises from a choice of coordinates and carries no
operational content for any internal observer.

VI. CONSEQUENCES FOR GENERAL
RELA TIVITY
This section summarizes the implications of the Rela-
tivistic Temporal Non-Constructibility Theorem for the
structure of general relativity. The results clarify several
long-standing conceptual issues—particularly the absence
of a physical global time, the role of the Hamiltonian con-
straint, and the timeless character of canonical quantum
gravity.
A. Absence of an Observable Global Time
General relativity contains no preferred global temporal
parameter. Coordinate timet enters only through a cho-
sen foliation of the spacetime manifold and its associated
lapse functionN(x,t ). [1] Section V established that this
parameter is operationally inaccessible to any internal
observer. Proper time along worldlines is invariant; the
foliation label is not.
Two immediate consequences follow:
i.Global simultaneity is not physical.
No observer can determine whether two spatially
separated events share the same value oft. [8]
ii.The “rate” at which the universe evolves cannot be
measured.
Rescaling t and N produces empirically identical
observational histories.
Thus the only temporal quantity with invariant content
in GR is the proper timeτ along a worldline. All global
time parameters are artifacts of coordinate choice.

Energy

eral relativity takes the form

where H and Hi are the Hamiltonian and momentum
constraints. The lapseN and shiftNi appear as Lagrange

The Relativistic TNT now provides the operational
reason for this structure:
•Nis a gauge variable.
• Changes inN correspond to reparametrizations of
the foliation parametert.
• Because N carries no observable information, the
physical Hamiltonian must not generate genuine

Hence the condition

of temporal gauge freedom. The Hamiltonian of GR must
vanish (up to constraints) precisely because the global
time parameter is not a physical observable.
This resolves the conceptual tension often associated
with “frozen-time” formulations of GR. The theory is not
frozen; rather, physical evolution is encoded in geometric
relations and proper time, not in coordinate time.

time.
• Therefore, its quantum counterpart cannot contain

• The equationHΨ = 0is the quantum expression of

Timelessness is not a failure of quantization; it is the
inevitable quantization of a theory whose global temporal

VII. LIMIT A TIONS
The results established in this paper concern the oper-
ational status of global time in classical general relativ-
ity. While the Temporal Non-Constructibility Theorem
provides a rigorous characterization of temporal gauge
freedom within the ADM framework, its scope is limited
in several important respects. These limitations clarify
the domain of validity of the theorem and delineate the
boundary for future extensions.
A. Restriction to Classical General Relativity
The analysis is confined to smooth Lorentzian space-
times satisfying Einstein’s field equations. Quantum ef-
fects, including those associated with quantum matter
fields or attempts to quantize the spacetime metric itself,
are not considered. The theorem does not address quan-
tum fluctuations of geometry or the behavior of temporal
gauge at the Planck scale. These issues are reserved for
subsequent work extending the framework to semiclassical
and canonical quantum gravity.
B. Requirement of Global Hyperbolicity
The ADM decomposition assumes that the spacetime
is globally hyperbolic. This ensures the existence of a
foliation by Cauchy surfaces and allows one to formulate
initial-value evolution equations in a well-defined way.
[4, 5] The theorem therefore does not apply to:
•spacetimes with closed timelike curves,
• non-globally hyperbolic geometries such as Anti-de
Sitter space without appropriate boundary condi-
tions,
•or pathological solutions lacking a Cauchy surface.
The conclusion that global time is unobservable holds
only within the class of spacetimes where foliation-based
dynamics is meaningful.
C. No Claim About the Existence or Non-Existence
of Time
The theorem does not assert that “time does not exist,”
nor does it imply that physical evolution is illusory. What
is shown is narrower and fully operational:
• the foliation parametert used to slice spacetime is
not accessible to any internal observer,
•the lapse functionN(x,t)is a gauge variable,
• and physical time corresponds to proper time along
worldlines.
The theorem concerns the non-constructibility of the
coordinate label, not the physical processes that unfold
along timelike paths.
D. No Implications for Discrete or Non-Manifold
Models
The argument relies on smooth manifold structure,
differentiability of fields, and the causal properties of
Lorentzian geometry. The theorem does not directly
apply to:

Within classical GR, no physically plausible extension
of observational capability circumvents the causal-past
constraint, but the theorem is restricted to the local
observer formulation.
F. Limitation to Coordinate-Time
Reparametrization
The theorem identifies the reparametrization of the foli-
ation labelt→t ′ =f(t)and its associated lapse transfor-
mation as the source of temporal gauge freedom. It does
not analyze more general refoliation symmetries involv-
ing changes in spatial coordinates or combined spacetime
diffeomorphisms beyond the class that preserves the foli-
ation structure. These general diffeomorphisms are well
understood in GR, but isolating the temporal component
requires the ADM setting used here.
G. No Claim About Relational Reformulations
Finally, the theorem does not propose an alternative
formulation of physics withoutt. It merely establishes
that the ADM foliation parameter cannot be observed.
A relational or renewal-based formulation of dynamics,
where evolution is indexed by invariant internal structure
rather than external time, requires further development.
Such formulations are the subject of subsequent papers.
VIII. CONCLUSION
This paper has established that in general relativity,
the global temporal parameter used to label the slices of
a foliation carries no operational meaning for an inter-
nal observer. By working entirely within the standard
Lorentzian and ADM frameworks, we demonstrated that
the lapse functionN(x,t )and the associated coordinate
time t are gauge artifacts: they determine how spacetime
is sliced and labeled, but they have no measurable effect
on the experience or recorded history of any physical
observer.
The central result, the Relativistic Temporal Non-
Constructibility Theorem, shows that for any two
foliations related by a smooth, strictly monotonic
reparametrization, all empirical data accessible to an ob-
server remain unchanged. Proper time along worldlines,
defined geometrically by the metric and invariant under
all diffeomorphisms, is the only operationally meaningful
temporal quantity. The observable history of an observer
depends solely on this invariant temporal structure and
on the geometric events encountered along the worldline,
not on the global slicing parameter used to evolve initial
data.
This conclusion clarifies several long-standing features
of general relativity. The Hamiltonian constraint
H≈0(30)
and the vanishing of the canonical Hamiltonian are direct
expressions of temporal gauge freedom. The timeless
form of the Wheeler-DeWitt equation likewise reflects the
fact that global time is not a physical degree of freedom.
In cosmology, the standard choice ofN = 1defining
“cosmic time” is a convenient gauge fixing rather than
an observable structure inherent to the universe. Across
all these contexts, this work identifies the underlying
reason: slicing-based time coordinates do not correspond
to operationally recoverable quantities.
The theorem provides a transparent bridge between the
classicalformulationoftheTemporalNon-Constructibility
Theorem, [2] proven in the context of autonomous dynam-
ical systems, and the relativistic generalization presented
here. In both settings, temporal parameters used to label
