1 Introduction
Quantum mechanics normally uses time as the parameter against which state evolution is written,
whereas general relativity makes clock rates local, path-dependent, and sensitive to the metric
environment [4, 5, 6]. The resulting problem is not only technical. It is ontological: is time a
universal parameter, a geometric coordinate, a thermodynamic ordering, an operational clock
reading, or something deeper?
This manuscript synthesizes the temporal sector of the MMA-DMF framework as developed
across the prior MMA-DMF relativity, gravitoelectromagnetism, and high-energy formulations
[1, 2, 3]. Its purpose is to state the ontology of time, connect it to the existing MMA-DMF
equation family, organize completed convergence layers, and identify the remaining decisive
falsification gates. The present paper does not depend on unreleased NASA or GNOME records
for formulation. Those records are external adjudication layers.
The core proposal is direct. Within MMA-DMF, time is not treated as a rigid background
metronome and not treated as an empty geometric container. Time is modeled as the local causal
relaxation of an active vacuum that carries memory, dissipates perturbations, and responds
differently in dense screened environments and low-density boundary environments. A clock is
then a local response system. It ticks according to the screened or unscreened relaxation state
of the physical vacuum surrounding and dressing its constituents. This is why the paper can
affirm the Einstein limit while still asking whether that limit is the final ontology of time.
The phrase Einstein limit is used carefully. It does not mean that standard relativistic
clock phenomenology is ignored. It means that, in dense screened conditions, MMA-DMF must
recover the same operational timing behavior that makes relativity indispensable in GPS/GNSS,
Shapiro timing, orbital mechanics, gravitational redshift and local Lorentz kinematics [7]. The
framework’s claim is not that known relativistic clocks are wrong. Its claim is that their
successful geometric description can be interpreted as the screened projection of deeper local
vacuum-response dynamics.
The paper is organized around four levels. First, the formal equation spine defines the
theory’s temporal sector. Second, the ontology interprets the equations: retarded memory
supplies temporal direction, density-dependent relaxation supplies local time, and fluctuation–
dissipation closure supplies thermodynamic stability. Third, completed convergence and bridge
layers are placed in a hierarchy rather than listed as one undifferentiated dossier. Fourth, the
strongest boundary-timing predictions are left where they belong: at the external falsification
gate.
This structure is intentionally different from a broad technical inventory. The manuscript
keeps the central thesis visible. FRB, Bell, GNOME, Voyager, GNSS, HETDEX, Technion and
gravitational-waveechoscreensarenotallmadetocarrythesameevidentialweight. Bellandhigh-
energy timing are completed convergence or bridge layers under explicit boundaries. GNOME
public products and Voyager LECP/PDS products are strong public and boundary context
layers. The decisive tests still require station-level GNOME timing streams and NASA/Voyager
DSN records. That distinction strengthens the manuscript because it prevents both overclaiming
and underclaiming.

2.1 Narrative hierarchy
The strongest version of the paper does not treat every test as an equal proof attempt. The
hierarchy used here is:
1. Temporal ontology: time is modeled as local causal relaxation of an active, memory-bearing
vacuum.
2. Equation spine: generalized Langevin dynamics, density-dependent effective mass, retarded
and even kernels, FDT closure, geometric relaxation and density-delay relations.
3. Einstein-limit recovery: ordinary clock and geometric observables are recovered as dense
screened projections.
4. Completed convergence and bridge layers: Bell, high-energy timing, GNOME
public products, Voyager LECP/PDS context, Technion/topological phase dynamics,
HETDEX/Lyα, GNSS source products and GW echo screens.
5. Remaining decisive falsification gates: NASA/Voyager DSN operational or radiometric
residual records and GNOME Science Run 2 station-level timing streams.
This hierarchy prevents a reader from mistaking a broad convergence map for a single validation
claim.
3 MMA-DMF Temporal Ontology
3.1 The answer in one sentence
Within MMA-DMF, time is the local retarded update of the active vacuum. This statement
has three parts. Local means that timing is generated by the state of the environment, not
by a universal master clock.Retarded means that the physical response depends on the past
light-cone and the causal history of the vacuum, not on advanced response.Update means that
time is the physically realized ordering of state changes, not an independent substance.
The active-vacuum and gravitoelectromagnetic interpretation follows the prior MMA-DMF
gravitoelectromagnetism formulation and the associated relativity closure [2, 1]. The framework

3.2 Clock rates as response rates
A clock is a physical oscillator. Atomic transition clocks, pulsar timing, spacecraft radiometric
tracking and laboratory interferometers all depend on material and field degrees of freedom. In
MMA-DMF, those degrees of freedom are dressed by the local scalar-vacuum response. A clock
rate is therefore not an abstract flow of time. It is the local rate at which the clock’s physical
constituents relax, oscillate and phase-accumulate under the environment-dependent response of
the vacuum.
Dense environments produce screened behavior. In those regimes the response is stiff and
the Einstein-limit projection is recovered. Low-density or transition regions can become more
sensitive to boundary terms, delayed relaxation or achromatic residuals. The ontology therefore
does not deny relativistic clocks. It gives them a mechanism: clocks tick as local vacuum-response
systems.
3.3 Arrow of time and memory
The arrow of time is treated as more than a coarse-grained entropy slogan. The theory uses
a retarded kernel in the dynamical equation. Because the kernel enters as a causal memory
integral, the future state depends on past deformations, while the reverse dependence is not
present. The even kernel used for equilibrium FDT normalization is not a second causal channel.
It is the autocorrelation object required to normalize noise consistently.
This distinction is central. Without it, the model would either risk acausal interpretation or
lose thermodynamic closure. With it, the arrow of time is tied to the same equations that define
stochastic response and dissipative relaxation.

5 Arrow of Time: Retarded Memory and FDT Closure
The arrow of time is often described statistically: entropy increases because high-entropy
macrostates dominate phase space. MMA-DMF does not need to reject that statement. It
instead adds a dynamical substrate beneath it. The retarded kernel in Eq.(5) makes the
response depend on previous deformations. The FDT closure in Eq.(7) prevents the stochastic
sector from injecting unlimited energy. Together they model temporal direction as a physical
memory-and-relaxation process.
The key point is that the arrow is not smuggled in by a hand-picked data cut. It is part of the
dynamical law. In a time-reversal operation, a memory integral over the past does not become
the same physical object unless the causal structure is also reversed. MMA-DMF therefore treats
macroscopic temporal direction as the visible limit of retarded vacuum response. Observationally,
this motivates sign tests: relaxation events should preferentially show negative chirp when the
template applies.
The even kernel in Eq.(6) is necessary because FDT relates dissipation to equilibrium
fluctuations. It would be wrong to read the even kernel as a bidirectional causal force. The
manuscript therefore uses two objects: a causal retarded kernel for dynamics and an even
autocorrelation kernel for equilibrium normalization. This is a technical detail, but it carries
ontological weight. It prevents the formalism from confusing thermodynamic symmetry with
physical advanced response.
6 Local Time and the Absence of a Master Clock
A master clock would assign a single global rate to the universe. MMA-DMF does not use such
a clock. The vacuum state near a detector, clock, spacecraft path or compact source determines
how the local response unfolds. Eq.(9) defines the relaxation rate; Eq.(11) defines relative
timing offsets, with Eq.(12) as its low-density approximation; Eq.(14) defines how a network
should be tested.
This is a stronger statement than ordinary time-zone or synchronization convention. It says
that even after ordinary coordinate conventions are fixed, the response time of the physical
medium is local. The model’s no-master-clock thesis becomes testable only when station-level
streams and environmental metadata are available. Public detector summaries can support
source, sensitivity and noise layers, but they cannot substitute for the station-level timing gate.
The decisive GNOME prediction is not that a public summary plot proves local time. It
is that, for a suitable event class, a density-delay corrected negative-chirp network likelihood
should outperform flat simultaneity. Conversely, if station-level data show equal or better
coherence without density delay, or if positive-chirp events survive the same controls, the

12 Discussion
The paper’s central improvement is editorial and scientific hierarchy. A weaker manuscript would
try to prove time, FRBs, Bell, GNOME, Voyager, GNSS, HETDEX and gravitational-wave
echoes all at once. This version does not. It puts the ontology first, the equations second, the
completed convergence layers third, and the decisive gates fourth.
The strongest conceptual claim is that time is not a primitive container. It is the causal
relaxation ordering of an active memory-bearing vacuum. The strongest mathematical claims
are the retarded kernel/FDT separation, density-dependent mass, geometric relaxation law
and Einstein-limit absorption. The strongest completed bridge layers are Bell CH/Eberhard,
high-energy negative-chirp convergence, GNOME public products, Voyager LECP/PDS context,
GNSS source products and topological/cosmological bridges under explicit boundaries.

local-time network prediction is weakened. If NASA/Voyager DSN records show no achromatic

the dynamic contextuality extension is weakened. If atom-interferometer density modulation
shows no signal below the claimed sensitivity, the laboratory density response is weakened.
The important point is that none of these possible failures prevents the framework from being
formulated. They decide whether its strongest timing-sector predictions survive. A theory that
can be formulated independently and then exposed to decisive external records is scientifically
stronger than a theory whose existence depends on finding one preferred anomaly.

A useful DSN gate would include S/X-band radiometric Doppler and range residuals, observed-

refit history. The test is not whether the Voyager heliopause exists; that boundary is already

H.2 GNOME station-level timing gate
A useful GNOME timing gate would include Science Run 2 station streams, sampling rates,
clock metadata, calibration, local noise, station shielding, environmental monitors and density

under density-delay correction. A weakening result would show equal or better coherence under
flat simultaneity, robust positive chirps passing controls, or absence of events in a claimed
sensitivity region.
H.3 Next-phase laboratory gates
The controlled fast-switch Bell roll-off, atom-interferometer density modulation, GNSS physical
residual fit and full GW matched-filter echo analysis are not required to define the temporal
ontology. They are useful next-phase discriminants. Their role is to increase or decrease
confidence in specific sectors without confusing formulation with validation.
