THE STABILITY OF MARGULIS SPACE-TIMES WITH
PARABOLIC HOLONOMY ELEMENTS
SUHYOUNG CHOI
Abstract. LetEbe a flat Lorentzian space of signature (2 , 1). A Margulis
space-time is a noncompact complete flat Lorentzian 3-manifoldE /Γ, where
the holonomy group Γ is a free group of rankg ≥ 2 acting freely and properly
discontinuously by isometries. We consider the case where Γ contains a parabolic
element. We show that sufficiently small deformations of Γ still act properly
discontinuously onEprovided their linear parts are Fuchsian; moreover, the
number of conjugacy classes of parabolic elements may increase or decrease
under deformation. Our proof combines our previous compactification ofE /Γ
relative to parabolic holonomy elements with a partial generalization of the
work of Carri` ere. However, this result depends only on the parts on parabolic
actions of our earlier work. We believe that the shortness of the proof of this
openness result is of independent interest.
1.Introduction
LetEdenote R2,1 viewed as an affine space with the standard coordinate system,
Lorentzian inner product, orientation, and time-orientation. Let Γ be a discrete free
subgroup of Isom(E) of rank n≥ 2 acting properly discontinuously and freely onE.
ThenE /Γ is said to be aMargulis space-time, and Γ is the holonomy group. (See
Section 2.2.)
Every element of Isom(E) is of the form x7→Ax +b for A∈SO (2, 1) and
b∈R 2,1. There is a homomorphism L : Isom(E)→SO (2, 1) taking the linear
part of the affine transformations. Denote by SO(2, 1)+ the subgroup of elements
preserving the future cone. We denote by Isom+(E) the inverse image of SO(2, 1)+
inIsom(E).
By Section 2.12 of [ 12],L|Γ is an injective map to a discrete group since a free
group of rank n≥ 2 is never virtually solvable. Since L(Γ) is a Fuchsian group, it
is well known that there can be only finitely many conjugacy classes of parabolic
elements. (See Section 2.3.)

elementsη1,...,η m can be compactified relative to these parabolic elements if we
can add a totally geodesic real projective surface and remove a union of disjoint solid
tori with these parabolic elements as holonomies to obtain a compact 3-manifold
whose interior is homeomorphic to M. (See Definition 2.2.) This is a fairly strong

2.2.Geometric structures on manifolds.A pair ( G,X ) of a Lie group G acting
on the space X faithfully and transitively is called the ( G,X )-geometry. A (G,X )-
structure on a manifold M is a maximal atlas of charts to X such that the transition
maps are inG.
The geometry ( Aff(Rn),Rn) for n≥ 1 is called theaffine geometry, and a
geometric structure on a manifold modeled on the pair is called anaffine structure.
The geometry ( Isom(E), E) is called theLorentz geometry, and a geometric
structure on a manifold modeled on the pair is called aflat Lorentzian structure.
Clearly, a flat Lorentzian structure give us a canonical affine structure of dimension
3.
The geometry ( SL±(n + 1,R ),Sn) for n≥ 1 is called thereal projective geometry,
and a geometric structure on a manifold modeled on the pair is called areal projective
structure.
We have inclusions
(Isom(E),E)⊂(Aff(R 3),R 3)⊂(SL ±(4,R),S 3).
Hence, a flat Lorentzian manifold, and so a Margulis space-time, can be considered
an affine 3-manifold and also a real projective 3-manifold.
LetN be a n-dimensional manifold with a universal cover ˜N and a deck transfor-
mation group π1(N). Given an affine (resp. projective) structure on N, there exists
an immersion dev : ˜N→X for X = Rn (resp. X = Sn) and a homomorphism
h : π1(N)→Aff (Rn) (resp. →SL±(n + 1,R )) satisfying dev◦γ = h(γ)◦dev
