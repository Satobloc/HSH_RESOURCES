A G_2G2-Holonomy Model for Late-Time Cosmic Acceleration in M-theory:
Alleviating the Hubble Tension through Geometric Vacuum Energy
Moustafa Amin M. Radwan

consistent across both cosmological and astrophysical scales. We demonstrate that a natural attractor solution alleviates the Hubble
