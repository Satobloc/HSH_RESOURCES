7.2EvolutionarySenescenceTheoryandCCT
Evolutionary senescence theory [2, 3] explains aging through declining selection
pressure with age — but cannot explain why larger animals evolved longer lifetimes
while smaller animals did not. If mutation accumulation were sufficient, smaller
animalsshouldhaveevolvedlongevityundersufficientselectionpressure.
CCT provides a complementary explanation: longevity in large animals is not a
"selected advantage" but a forced time-cost of deep nesting under the LEEC
conservation constraint. Deep nesting's high maintenance cost forces master rhythm
down-regulation; low frequency itself is the direct cause of longevity. Evolution
selects the functional advantages of deep nesting (brains, long-distance locomotion);
longevityisastructuralbyproduct.
7.3FreeEnergyPrinciple(FEP)
The FEP [5] offers a unified account of self-organization: systems minimize
variational free energy by optimizing the accuracy–complexity trade-off. CCT Θ₀
maps to the FEP generative model's prior confidence; Γ maps to the complexity term
accumulation rate; Θ_crit maps to the minimum prediction error threshold for
self-organization.
However, CCT provides what FEP lacks: temporal-scale dynamics. FEP describes
"what the best inference is"; CCT describes "how long the best inference persists."
They are complementary: FEP addresses inference space, CCT addresses persistence
time.
Crucially, the two frameworks differ in their treatment of suppression: FEP treats
emotional suppression as a strategy to minimize prediction error (a form of "action"
that changes sensory input). CCT treats it as a malignant increase in internal
decoherence rate — suppression may reduce immediate prediction error, but it
accelerates the long-term decoherence of the constraint closure, shortening lifetime.
This is a key divergence: FEP is synchronic (minimizing current free energy), while
CCTisdiachronic(trackingcumulativedecoherenceovertime).

9.Discussion
9.1CoreContribution
The CCT theorem demonstrates that lifetime is not a biological concept but a
universal temporal attribute of constraint closures. The unified criterion T_life =
Θ₀/Γ(N) applies across the organic–inorganic divide. Both living systems
("frequency reduction for life extension," Γ_bio = Γ₀·e^(−αN) + γ₀/N) and
non-living systems ("barrier elevation for lifespan preservation," Γ_phys =
Γ₀·e^(−λN)) are two symmetric branches of a single ontological constraint:
conservationofindividualΘ-potentialtransformation.
9.2TheoreticalSignificance
Thetheoremimplies:
1. No ontological discontinuity between living and non-living — both obey the
same temporaldynamics; the difference isonly inthe functional form ofΓ(N).
2. Time is not a uniformly flowing river but the externalization of internal
coherence within constraint closures. Deeply nested systems have slower
"clocks" (living) or thicker "walls" (non-living) — the universe grants longer
existence windowsto more deeply nested structures.
3. Heart rate is only a proxy, not the fundamental criterion — the fundamental
criterion is the generalized decoherence rate Γ_eff, which includes both physical
and internal entropygeneration.

9.3Limitations
Current precision: All cross-scale estimates are B-level (directional criterion, error
about ±1 log unit for lifetimes, ±2 log units for the ln(Θ₀/Θ_crit) factor). A-level
quantification requires independent scale-by-scale calibration of Θ₀, Θ_crit, and
Γ₀.
Environmental fluctuations: The theorem assumes stable T_env. Large
environmentalperturbations(supernovae,asteroidimpacts,catastrophicclimateshifts)

Scale-law limitation: Cross-level N comparisons (chemical vs. biological vs.
astrophysical) lack normalized scaling-law foundation. This boundary is explicitly
statedinSection6.3.
Distinguishing benign from malignant deceleration: In living systems, empirical
separation of Γ_pump from Γ_internal remains challenging. Biological age markers
(epigeneticclocks,inflammatorymarkers)mayprovidetheneededdistinction.

internal decoherence.
 Civilizational persistence: Civilizational survival depends not on accelerating
information production but on increasing the depth of knowledge
purification/compression (increasing N ofextra-somatic I-structures).
