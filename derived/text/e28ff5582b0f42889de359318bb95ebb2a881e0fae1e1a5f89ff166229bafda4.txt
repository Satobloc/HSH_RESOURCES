Isomorphism for the Holonomy Group
of a K-Contact Sub-Riemannian Space
E. A. Kokin
Abstract. The holonomy group of the adapted connection on a K-contact R iemannian man-
ifold (M, θ, g) is considered. It is proved that if the orbit space M/ξ of the Reeb ﬁeld ξ action
admits a manifold structure, then the holonomy group of the a dapted connection on M is
isomorphic to the holonomy group of the Levi-Civita connect ion on the Riemannian manifold
(M/ξ, h), where h is the induced Riemannian metric on M/ξ. Thanks to this result, a simple
proof of the de Rham theorem for the case of K-contact sub-Rie mannian manifolds is obtained,
stating that if the holonomy group of the adapted connection on M is not irreducible, then the
orbit space M/ξ is locally a product of Riemannian manifolds.
Keywords: sub-Riemannian manifold of contact type, adapted connecti on, de Rham de-
composition, holonomy group

Introduction
Nonholonomic, contact, and sub-Riemannian manifolds occu py an important place in modern
diﬀerential geometry. These spaces have numerous applicat ions both in geometry and mathe-
matical physics, geometric control theory, and analysis on manifolds with constraints given by
nonintegrable distributions. Recently, particular atten tion has been paid to the study of con-
nections naturally adapted to the sub-Riemannian and conta ct structure, see for example the
monographs [ 1, 5, 8]. In particular, the adapted connection allows the study of global and local
geometric properties of contact-type sub-Riemannian mani folds, such as curvature characteristics,
Ricci and Einstein tensors, as well as questions related to h olonomy groups.
We describe the content and results of the present article. I n Section 1 the basics of holonomy
group theory of Riemannian manifolds and the de Rham theorem are given. In Section 2 K-contact
sub-Riemannian manifolds, the adapted connection and its h olonomy group are deﬁned. Section
3 gives a version of de Rham’s theorem for K-contact sub-Riem annian manifolds recently proved
in [ 13]. Section 4 contains the central result of this paper, descr ibing the relationship between
the holonomy group of the adapted connection of a K-contact s ub-Riemannian manifold and the
holonomy group of the Levi-Civita connection of the induced metric on the orbit space of the
Reeb ﬁeld action under the assumption that the orbit space ad mits a natural smooth manifold
structure. An example of the contactization of a Kähler mani fold is considered. The obtained
isomorphism of holonomy groups allowed to give in Section 5 a simple proof of de Rham’s theorem
for K-contact sub-Riemannian manifolds. An example of an S1-bundle over a symmetric Kähler
space is considered.

3. De Rham Theorem for K-Contact Sub-Riemannian Manifolds
If the holonomy group Hol x(∇A) ⊂ O(Dx,g x) of the adapted connection is not irreducible,
then by complete reducibility Hol x(∇A) preserves an orthogonal decomposition
(5) TxM =E1 ⊕ · · · ⊕Er.
The Reeb vector ﬁeld ξ deﬁnes a pseudogroup of diﬀeomorphisms of the manifold M . Denote
byM/ξ the orbit space of this pseudogroup. In general, the space M/ξ has a complicated structure
and is not a smooth manifold. However, each point of M has an open neighborhood U such that
U/ξ admits a natural manifold structure and there is a surjectiv e submersion
pr : U →U/ξ,
whose diﬀerential restricted to the layers of the contact di stribution
dxpr : DxU →Tpr(x)U/ξ
is an isomorphism of vector spaces. In this case, since the ma nifold is K-contact, the sub-
Riemannian metric g|U deﬁnes a Riemannian metric on U/ξ which we denote by hU .
The main result of [ 13] is the following theorem generalizing the local de Rham the orem for
Riemannian manifolds.
Theorem 2. Let (M,D,g ) be a K-contact oriented sub-Riemannian manifold. Assume
that the holonomy group of the adapted connection preserves t he decomposition (5). Then for each
point of M there is an open neighborhood U ⊂ M such that the manifold (U/ξ,h U ) is isometric
to the product of Riemannian manifolds
(B1,g 1) × · · · × (Br,g r).

5. Proof of de Rham’s Theorem for K-Contact Sub-Riemannian M anifolds
In this section we give a simple proof of theorem 2. Suppose th e holonomy group Hol x(∇A) of
the adapted connection preserves the decomposition (5). Ob viously, for every open subset W ⊂M
such that x ∈M we have an inclusion
Hol x(∇A|W ) ⊂ Hol x(∇A),
and consequently the holonomy group Hol x(∇A|W ) preserves the decomposition (5). Let U be
an open neighborhood of x such that U/ξ is a manifold. By theorem 3 the holonomy group
Hol x(∇A|U ) is isomorphic to the holonomy group Hol p(∇h|U/ξ ), therefore Hol p(∇h|U/ξ ) pre-
serves the decomposition (2), where
Es = (dxpr)Fs, s = 1,...,r.
By theorem 2 there is an open subset V ⊂ U/ξ such that the Riemannian manifold (V,h V ) is
isometric to the product of Riemannian manifolds. By shrink ing U if necessary, we can assume
U/ξ =V . This proves theorem 2.
Example 2. Consider the product of compact Kähler symmetric spaces
(N,h ) = (N1,h 1) × · · · × (Nr,h r).
As above, the holonomy group of (N,h ) is the product of the holonomy groups of the factors. It is
known [ 4] that the S1-bundle obtained from the canonical bundle over (N,h ) is a sub-symmetric
K-contact sub-Riemannian manifold (M,θ,g ). According to theorem 3 there is an isomorphism
Hol x(∇A) ∼= Hol pr(x)(∇h).
The de Rham decomposition for (M,θ,g ) coincides with the original decomposition for (N,h ).
