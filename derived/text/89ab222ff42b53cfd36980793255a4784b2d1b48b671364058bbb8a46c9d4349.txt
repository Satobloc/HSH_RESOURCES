B.Boundary conditions 
 
For a fluid-fluid boundary (all that we will consider in this course), we have two 
boundary conditions that we must enforce. They are: 1) pressure is continuous across a 
boundary (so that there is no net force or acceleration of the boundary) and 2) the vertical 
component of particle displacement is continuous (so that there is no separation of the 
boundar
