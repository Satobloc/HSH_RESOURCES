1.Introduction
1.1Acentury-oldparadigmanditsfatalboundary
SinceEhrenfest(1933)proposedtheclassicalclassificationofphasetransitions,physicshasreliedona
fundamental premise: one must be able to write down the free-energy function of the system. First-order
transitions are defined by a discontinuous jump in the first derivative of the free energy; second-order
transitions by a divergence or jump in the second derivative. Combined with the renormalization group
(Wilson, 1971), this framework gave birth to the concept of "universality"—critical exponents depend only
onspatialdimensionalityandthesymmetryoftheorderparameter,notonmicroscopicdetails.
Yet this paradigm has a fatal boundary. The partition function of the 3D Ising model—the standard
model for real ferromagnets—was rigorously proved to be NP-complete (Istrail, 2000). The pairing

===== PAGE 3 =====
3
mechanism of high-temperature superconductors, glass transitionsin non-equilibrium systems—these fields
producevastamountsofdataforwhichtheEhrenfestclassificationoffersnoguidance.
A deeper problem exists: even on exactly solvable systems, universality holds only in the
thermodynamic limit (L→∞). In any real, finite system, the partition function is a finite sum of analytic
exponentials—and a finite sum of analytic functions isitselfanalytic. Hence, a finitesystem cannot possess
non-analytic singularities or genuine divergences. Universality is an ideal in the mathematician's hands, not
arealityintheexperimenter's.
1.2TheFactorHierarchyLawandtheTestabilityNorms
Tang (2026g) proposed the Factor Hierarchy Law: in any complex system driven by multiple
quantifiable factors, factors naturally fall into two tiers—regime factors and executive factors. A regime
factor determines which set of rules is currently active; its function is not reflected in direct explanatory
power but in how it modulates the exposure coefficients of executive factors through interaction effects.
Whenaregimefactorcrossesacriticalvalue,theweightsofallfactorsinthesystemaresystematicallyreset.
This law has been independently verified across five major financial markets (Tang, 2026a–2026f) and five
independentdimensionsinastronomy(Tang,2026h,2026i,2026j).
The Testability Norms (Tang, 2026g) are the operational realization of the law, comprising six
functional dimensions—search completeness, modulation relation, structural break, causal direction,
problem completeness, and process closed-loop—together with a set of replaceable implementation tools.
Tang (2026k) proved the exact information-theoretic duality between the Factor Hierarchy Law and the
Ehrenfest classification on the exactly solved 2D Ising model: the Chow test attains a unique global
maximum at the exact Tc, and the deviation is proved analytically to be strictly zero. Tang (2026l)
discovered hidden structures of finite-lattice phase transitions on the 3D Ising model (an NP-complete
system)—bimodal splitting, the transition regime, and the precursor regime boundary—and distilled the
Multi-RegimeStateDecoupling(MRSD)algorithm.

3.2Themathematicaloriginof χ-blindness
The Chow behavior of χ is examined on the exact 2D Ising solution (Table 3.1). Near Tc, χ(T) is a
symmetric even function. The Chow test compares the regression coefficients of two groups of data. For
χ(T), the shapes of the curve on either side of Tc are symmetric about Tc; the slopes on the two sides are
equal in magnitude and equal in sign, so the two regressionsare nearly identical and the Chow F is close to
zero. The residual F=16.8 arises from minor asymmetry in the data-point distribution, not from a structural
break—confirming thatχ is indeed blind to the regime switch. For M(T), the slopes on the two sides have
opposite signs (in the ferromagnetic phase M rises as T decreases, while in the paramagnetic phase M falls
as T increases), and the Chow test immediately detects this reversal. The function 1/χ(T) forms a V-shaped
cuspatTc—theslopechangesabruptlyfromnegativetopositive—andtheChowFexplodesto~10²¹.

7.2Thereductionofuniversality

physicallawtoastatisticalapproximationvalidonlyintheL→∞limit.
Proposition 1 (A phase transition is a zone, not a point). In a finite system, different physical
quantities complete their regime switches at different temperatures. On 3D Ising L=64, M_sp locks at 4.50,
