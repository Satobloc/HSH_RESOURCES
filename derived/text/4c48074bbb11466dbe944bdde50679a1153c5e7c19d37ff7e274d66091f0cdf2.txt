A. Derivation of basic PE 
Let’s look at a very clever and popular way to solve the acoustic wave equation, called 
the “parabolic equation (PE)” method. This is an “old method” now (transcribed for 
underwater acoustics from Russian electromagnetics work by Fred Tappert in 1977), but 
it is still being improved upon today, and is the workhorse for most lower frequency 
acoustics calculations. 
We start out with “standard forms” for a second order differential equation in (x,y) or 
(r,z) as we will get to soon.
