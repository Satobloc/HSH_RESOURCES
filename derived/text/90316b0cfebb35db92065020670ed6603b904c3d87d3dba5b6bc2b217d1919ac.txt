According to this hypothesis, dark energy could be a natural effect of quantum
gravity acting on the geometry of spacetime itself.
Quantum gravity and dark energy are among the biggest unsolved problems in
physics. The former seeks to describe gravity when quantum effects become
relevant, while the latter is the name given to the component thought to be
responsible for the accelerating expansion of the universe.
