Abstract
This paper proposes a geometric Unified Field Theory where all fundamental interactions, mediator
particles, and vacuum fluctuations are treated as manifestations of space-time curvature. We establish a
direct link between the Ricci scalar curvature (R) and the nature of forces, and introduce a quantized
space-time structure that selectively filters particles based on their volumetric oscillation amplitudes.
Keywords: Unified field theory; Ricci scalar curvature; quantized space-time; graviton; photon; gluon;
weak interaction; General Relativity; Quantum Gravity; black hole thermodynamics.

forces.

9. Discussion
The research presented in this paper establishes a novel geometric framework that unifies fundamental
interactions through the intrinsic curvature of space-time. By refining the field equations to explicitly
distinguish between attractive and repulsive geometric tendencies, we provide a coherent explanation for
phenomena ranging from quark confinement to cosmic expansion.
9.1 The Geometric Balance of Forces
A cornerstone of this update is the formulation of the Unified Field Equation as a superposition of
competing curvature components ( R = Rattract + Rrepulse). Unlike standard General Relativity, where
curvature is primarily associated with mass-energy attraction, our model generalizes geometry to include:
 Concave Geometry (R< 0): Universally describing attractive potentials, including Gravity, Strong/Weak
nuclear forces, Dark Matter, and the electromagnetic force between two opposite charges.
 Convex Geometry ( R > 0): Describing repulsive effects, effectively unifying Dark Energy and
electromagnetic repulsion under a single geometric sign.
This classification eliminates the need for ad hoc “exotic matter” to explain Dark Energy, interpreting it
instead as the natural manifestation of the Rrepulse component dominating on cosmological scales.
9.2 Tensor Formulation and General Relativity Recovery
A significant theoretical advance in this work is the derivation of the Einstein-like Effective Field Equa-
tion . By defining a Unified Oscillation Tensor based on the amplitude-frequency relation, we bridge the
gap between the scalar geometry of our unified potential and the tensorial precision of Einstein’s equations.
This formulation ensures that:
1. At macroscopic scales (L≫ℓP ), where quantum gaps smooth out (δquantum→ 0), the theory naturally
recovers General Relativity as a low-energy effective approximation.
2. At microscopic scales, the tensor trace recovers the quantum energy density, validating the consistency
of the model with quantum mechanics.
