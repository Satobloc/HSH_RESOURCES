Quantum measurement influences the past, with
Avshalom Elitzur
Time and consciousness in quantum physics
18th August 2026
We think that the future follows the past;
causes preceed effects. But theoretical
physicist Avshalom Elitzur argues that
quantum physics alters our conception of
time and causation: a measurement done in
the present influences not only the future, but
the past. In this conversation, he explores the
radical implications of quantum theory for the
nature of time and consciousness, as well as
what the future holds for physics.
