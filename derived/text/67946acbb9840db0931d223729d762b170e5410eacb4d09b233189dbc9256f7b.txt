Quantum Magic Reveals CP Phases Invisible
to Entanglement in Spin-0 Decays
Nicolas Viaux, Ariel Norambuena, Pedro Orellana
View PDF HTML (experimental)
All standard scalar quantum-information measures -- concurrence,
negativity, entanglement entropy, the optimized CHSH bound, and quantum
Fisher information -- are CP-blind in ideal \\ spin-0  decays because
the two-qubit spin state is maximally entangled for every CP angle. We show
that stabilizer magic, ﬁxed in the physical Pauli frame of spin analysis,
escapes this blind spot: the stabilizer Rényi entropy admits an exact closed
form, vanishing at CP-deﬁnite and Clifford phases and peaking at maximal
non-Clifford mixing. Two experimentally accessible, magic-inspired CP
witnesses follow; the linear amplitude is  more efﬁcient than its
quartic counterpart and reaches discovery-level sensitivity at the HL-LHC for
.
