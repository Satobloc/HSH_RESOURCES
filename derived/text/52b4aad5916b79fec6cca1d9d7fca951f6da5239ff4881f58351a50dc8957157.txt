1.Introduction
1.1TheTransit-DepthPrecisionProblem:AGapThatGrowsWithEveryFix
Exoplanet science faces a disturbing paradox.As space telescopes such as Kepler, K2, andTESS have
accumulated vast quantities of transit data, astronomers have found that the measurement uncertainty of
transit depth has not converged but instead systematically deviates from photon-noise expectations. The
recently articulated “Transit-Depth Precision Problem” (TDPP) explicitly states that actual errors can be
amplified to over ten times the theoretical value, and this amplification cannot be explained by existing
noisemodels.
Wang & Espinoza (2022) analyzed 28 known planets and showed that transit depths measured by
different facilities or observers exhibit significant differences. Morello et al. (2020), in multi-wavelength
transit observations using Spitzer and HST,found significant transit depth differences between instruments,
with the prevailing interpretation attributing these to stellar activity and inadequacies in limb-darkening
models. The astronomical community's response has been to adopt more complex limb-darkening (LD)
models, add fitting parameters, and employ higher-order polynomials. However, the core logic of these
approaches remains one of “correction”—treating the differences between facilities as systematic
errorsthatneedtobecalibratedaway.
1.2AQuestionThatHasNeverBeenAsked
This paper proposes an entirely different diagnosis. We do not ask “how to better correct for facility
differences.”Instead,weaskamorefundamentalquestion:Shouldfacilitydifferencesbecorrectedatall?

1.3TheoreticalFramework:FactorHierarchyTheoryandthePrincipleofTestability
The methodological foundation of this study comes from the Principle of Testability and Factor
Hierarchy Theory proposed by Tang (2026g). The theory posits that in any complex system driven by
multiple quantifiable factors, factors naturally fall into two hierarchical levels: regime factors determine
which set of rules currently applies, and executing factors operate under the given rules.The function of a
regime factor is not reflected in its direct explanatory power, but rather in the interaction effects through
which it modulates the exposure coefficients of executing factors. When a regime factor crosses a critical
threshold,thefactorweightsoftheentiresystemaresystematicallyreset.
Tang (2026g) discovered in finance that the China-U.S. interest rate spread is not a traditional pricing
factor (its direct R² is near zero) but rather a regime factor that determines how executing factors such as
VIX and DXY explain asset prices. When the spread crosses the zero axis, the entire pricing equation
undergoes a structural break. This theory has been fully tested across five major financial markets, but
independentcross-disciplinaryverificationremainspending.
At the level of philosophy of science, Tang (2026g) points out that Popper's falsifiability stipulates
the entry criterion for scientific knowledge—a proposition must be capable of being overturned by facts.
However, the classic criticism of Popper's theory within philosophy of science has always focused on its
“attitude principle without operational procedures”: Popper required scientists to possess the spirit of
falsification, but he never specified “what counts as rigorous testing” in concrete empirical research. Tang

(2026g)proposesthatscience requiresa secondthreshold—testability:aproceduralstandardthatspecifies
the concrete steps of testing—exhaustive search replaces prior selection, interaction effects and Chow tests
replace the assumption of coefficient stability, and multiple verification replaces single significance.
Falsifiabilityguardstheentranceofscience,testabilityguardstheexitofscientificdiscovery.Together,they
constitutethecompletechainofscientificmethodology.
1.45W2HandPDCA:CompletenessCompanionstotheTestingProcedures
The Four-Test Method of Tang (2026g) specifies the procedures of testing—exhaustive search,
interaction effects, Chow tests, and auxiliary evidence. But procedures alone do not guarantee
completeness—how does one judge whether a research question has been exhausted in all its dimensions?
How does one judge whether the research process forms a closed loop? Tang (2026g) reserved an interface
atthemethodologicallevelfor“completenesscompanionstothetestingprocedures.”
Thispaper isthefirsttointroducethe 5W2H and PDCA frameworksfrom managementscience asthe
concrete implementation of this interface. 5W2H (What, Why, Who, Where, When, How, How Much)
ensures that the research question iscompletely covered across seven dimensions; PDCA(Plan, Do, Check,
Act) ensures that the research process completes the full Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle. The three together
constitutethecompleteoperationalsystemofthePrincipleofTestability: theFour-TestMethodprovidesthe
testingprocedures, 5W2Hprovidesthestandard ofquestioncompleteness,andPDCAprovidesthestandard
