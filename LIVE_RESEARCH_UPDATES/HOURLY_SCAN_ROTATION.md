## Retained in the latest pass

- Ruxi Shi, “A sharp embedding theorem for topological dynamical systems,” arXiv:2609.05664 v1, submitted 2026-09-04. For a topological dynamical system, generic n-component observation maps encode the full bi-infinite orbit injectively when Meyerovitch dynamical dimension is strictly below n/2; the factor 1/2 and strict inequality are optimal.
- Jason J. Bramburger, Shiloh-Sheray Gagnon, Rachel Morris, and Matthew J. Colbrook, “The Spectral Skeleton of Chaos: Koopman Wave Packets on Poincaré Sections,” arXiv:2609.06565 v1, submitted 2026-09-06. Finite Koopman wave packets recover coarse transport from sampled section crossings. In Duffing Regime II a seven-region rule explains 91–94% of filtered one-step transitions, while failures are 5.08–5.20 times more frequent in the lowest retained modulus decile.
- Anthony Conway, “Unknotting orientable surfaces,” arXiv:2609.06544 v1, submitted 2026-09-06. Every locally flat orientable surface in S4 is unknotted exactly when its knot group is Z; the new work closes genus 1 and 2 and supplies a relative-boundary isotopy result in D4.
- Niklas C. Affolter and Luis Mühlhoff, “Möbius Invariant Dimers and Miquel Dynamics,” arXiv:2609.07485 v1, submitted 2026-09-07. A circle-pattern/dimer correspondence is PO(3,1)-invariant, Miquel dynamics preserve its partition function, convex t-embeddings give positive weights, and a hidden PO(3,3) symmetry enters through a Lorentz lift.
- Pedro Olivares-Sánchez et al., “Knots, black holes, databases, and birthdays: Collision entropy of knot invariants,” arXiv:2609.08298 v1, submitted 2026-09-08. In two random-diagram models Alexander-polynomial and determinant collisions decay only as O(n^-1/2); database collisions therefore reappear on a birthday scale even when pairwise classification is asymptotically accurate.
