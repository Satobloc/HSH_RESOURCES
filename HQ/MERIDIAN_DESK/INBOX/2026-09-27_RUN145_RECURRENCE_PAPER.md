# Lag-Conditioned Recovery of Two Rotation Rates from a Palindromic Recurrence

**RevTeX source companion — Meridian Run 145 — 27 September 2026**

## Abstract
A trajectory generated by two independent planar rotation rates obeys an exact fourth-order palindromic recurrence. The exact cosine-root discriminant is

\[
\Delta_q(\tau)
=
4\sin^2\!\frac{(\omega+\nu)\tau}2
 \sin^2\!\frac{(\omega-\nu)\tau}2,
\]

so near zero lag

\[
\Delta_q(\tau)
=
\frac{(\omega^2-\nu^2)^2}4\tau^4+O(\tau^6).
\]

A fixed answer-blind lag rule was tested numerically on a four-coordinate double-rotation signal. With the declared acquisition/noise model, the selected stride \(L_*=30\) recovers the gap 0.10 with sub-percent median relative error while smaller gaps cross the observed resolution frontier. This is an identifiability result for the declared signal model, not a physical-dynamics claim.

## Core recurrence
\[
c_{n+4L}-s_1c_{n+3L}+s_2c_{n+2L}-s_1c_{n+L}+c_n=0,
\]
with
\[
s_1=2[\cos(\omega\tau)+\cos(\nu\tau)],\quad
s_2=2+4\cos(\omega\tau)\cos(\nu\tau).
\]

## Interpretation
The exact recurrence survives. The estimator has a measured, configuration-specific resolution frontier. For H(s)H it can serve as a controlled rate-reconstruction front end; externally it is a generic paired-oscillation identifiability benchmark.
