# Topology Is Not a Particle Label: A Typed-State Requirement for a Filament Particle Taxonomy

**RevTeX source companion — Meridian Run 145 — 27 September 2026**

## Source-derived result

The recovered historical Particle Zoo source explicitly makes **flavor a resonance mode** while grouping multiple flavors into the same broad filament class. It also assigns bosons to ripple/transition objects and baryons to braided composites.

Therefore a source-faithful solver needs at least

\[
X=(C,F,G,E,B,P),
\]

for carrier class, framing/topology, geometry, excitation/phase, binding/composition, and persistence/event type.

The particle label cannot be recovered injectively from carrier topology alone.

## Calculated representative

\[
\gamma_n(s)=\left(R\cos ns,R\sin ns,ps\right)
\]

has the same interval carrier topology for all positive integers \(n\), but

\[
\kappa_n=\frac{Rn^2}{R^2n^2+p^2},
\qquad
\tau_n=\frac{pn}{R^2n^2+p^2}
\]

change with harmonic number.

The exact three-strand braid representative is

\[
\beta_j(z)=
\left(
R_b\cos(2\pi z+2\pi j/3),
R_b\sin(2\pi z+2\pi j/3),
z
\right),
\]

with matched-\(z\) strand separation \(\sqrt3R_b\).

## Scope

This establishes a taxonomy constraint and a reproducible geometry implementation. It does **not** establish that these historical particle identifications are physically realized, nor does it assign a unique braid/knot invariant to every particle.
