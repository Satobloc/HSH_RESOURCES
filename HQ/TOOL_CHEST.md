## Standing deputy rule
At handoff, reboot, succession, or appointment of a second-in-command, explicitly point them here. Do not assume they know the machinery exists merely because they can access the repository.

If a tool is missing, broken, duplicated, dangerously opaque, or discoverable only by folklore: fix the map, fix the tool, or leave a durable note for the next person.
