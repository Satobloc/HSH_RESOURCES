# VEY-020 Amendment 02 | Copy-weight calibration boundary

Date: 2026-10-31  
Applies to: VEY-020 section 2.1 and public publisher issue #1152.

## Correction and qualification

The reported copy-weight (w_m) is reconstructed from the paper's posterior-equivalent evidence-unit estimate using log-odds. It is therefore a behavioral estimate derived from the aggregator's verbalized probability, not a directly observed internal belief or a universal model constant.

The displayed evidence-unit means are rounded. The recomputed values (0.275, 0.060, 0.210, 0.420) are approximate reconstructions from those rounded means. No raw per-trial output repository or executable analysis code for arXiv:2610.06192 was located in the paper, so these values have not been independently re-estimated from trial-level data.

The quantitative copy weights depend on the calibration and comparability of verbalized probabilities across the copy conditions. The paper's conflict probes and emitted-action channel provide additional qualitative evidence that repeated statements can affect commitment without relying solely on the log-odds conversion. Therefore:

- **Qualitative finding:** repeated copies can influence aggregation and early commitment in the tested controlled settings.
- **Quantitative finding:** the reported model-specific copy weights are calibration-dependent posterior-equivalent estimates.
- **Generalization:** neither the numeric weights nor the controlled provenance assumptions should be generalized to arbitrary documents, natural human groups, or sources with unknown shared failure modes.

This qualification does not reverse the reported reference-rule effect. It narrows the epistemic status of the numerical (w_m) estimates.

No new data, model inference, human-subject session, or calculation was performed for this amendment.
