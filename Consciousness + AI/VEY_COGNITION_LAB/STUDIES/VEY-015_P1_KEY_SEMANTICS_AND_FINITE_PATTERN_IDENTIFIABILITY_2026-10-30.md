### 4.1 Latent-to-observable map in OV-55/56

For n labelled agents and success pattern S⊆{1,...,n}, the shared-gate toy model assigns:

    w_G(S) = q * 1[S = empty] + (1-q) * p^|S| * (1-p)^(n-|S|).

The latent-difficulty toy model assigns probability q to a zero-success state and 1-q to independent per-agent correctness probability p. It yields:

    w_D(S) = q * 0^|S| * 1^(n-|S|) + (1-q) * p^|S| * (1-p)^(n-|S|).

Since 0^|S| * 1^(n-|S|) = 1[S = empty], the two full labelled-pattern weight functions are equal. For p,q∈[0,1], each is nonnegative and sums to one over the complete finite powerset.

The latent labels therefore map to the same observable finite distribution. The observation map is non-injective within this model family. Passive pattern frequencies alone cannot identify which of these two causal stories is intended. This is a model-conditional identifiability result, not evidence that either mechanism generated the archived LLM data.
