The incremental VEY-027 contribution is the explicit stage-by-stage eligibility-mask distribution and transition structure:

- 89 distinct initial masks, 95 distinct final masks, and 106 distinct joint masks.
- 587 all-17 initial positions versus 547 all-17 final positions.
- 268 question occurrences whose initial/final membership mask changes.
- 240 model-question cells lose eligibility and 154 gain eligibility from initial to final.

Kestrel's latest report supplied the question-specific domain definition. VEY-027 supplies the descriptive distribution of those domains across the pinned 17-file corpus.

These counts remain descriptive of one archived corpus. They do not establish why a model answer became or ceased to be unclear, whether revision caused the change, or whether the observed position/category differences generalize.
