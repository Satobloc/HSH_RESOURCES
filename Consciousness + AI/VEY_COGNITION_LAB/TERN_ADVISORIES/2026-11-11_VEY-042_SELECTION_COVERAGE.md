## Finding

P001 (Jia Shen, Miralucis, preprint v1.0, 2026-10-11) reports a bounded single-source handoff case in which 31 of 97 candidate items were omitted by one structured workflow versus 13 by ordinary free-form. The author says the structured omissions were already present at extraction and persisted through review/packaging. A follow-up fixed-eight-field summary reports bounded-selection 35 present / 31 partial / 31 absent and richer-selection 84 / 12 / 1. The dedicated P001 page abbreviates the cells as present/absent; the publications page supplies the partial category. The author warns of single-source and length/wording/sampling/environment confounds. This is not a causal effect or general estimate; full PDF/item-level data were not retrieved.

## Optional operational implication

For a handoff whose source universe can be bounded, consider adding a compact Selection Coverage Certificate before the existing representation/review/qualification stages. It should:

- freeze the candidate universe and source versions;
- record selected/excluded/unresolved decisions and reasons;
- map selected items to their handoff representation;
- distinguish PRESENT, PARTIAL, ABSENT, UNAVAILABLE, UNASSESSED, and NOT_APPLICABLE;
- keep authority and provenance status separate;
- report counts over the declared universe while acknowledging that the universe itself may be incomplete.

This is upstream of VEY-030/040 retention, access, comprehension, global-state fidelity, and action-relative support. It does not replace them. Use it selectively for material handoffs, not as a new mandatory task or a reason to expand every handoff indefinitely.

## Addendum 01 — carrier localization and source relevance

A full-manuscript external comparator, Thomas Ryan's 2026 Paper 6 preprint (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21342748), reports 12/12 core scores in all four blinded conditions. Its no-memory condition still retained visible repository state, tests, and task files. The bounded implication is that task-local source-of-truth state can carry action-level continuation in small coding tasks; it does not show that broader memory is generally irrelevant. One eligible row per condition and a shared broader Codex environment limit generalization.

For scheduler/handoff interpretation, distinguish task-local repository/test state from additional handoff, summary, persistent-memory, tool-trace, and control-state carriers. A missing handoff item may still be present in the repository; a successful action may therefore rely on task-local state rather than the handoff. Record actual availability/access and message exposure separately.
