# Structural SATity X-ray — corrected authority protocol

## Authority rule

**Nathan alone determines what SAT/H(s)H is, what it is not, why, and what counts as an "SAT move" in external physics.**

No model-authored rubric, inferred feature list, frequency analysis, clustering result, or external comparison may define SAT/H(s)H by itself. LLM judgment is not an authority source for SAT identity.

The purpose of this project is narrower and operational: produce a stable, LLM-legible representation of Nathan's own distinctions so that later comparisons use the same referent he intends.

## Current status

The prior 30-dimension scoring rubric was introduced prematurely by the model and is **not authorized as a SATity classifier**. The first scoring pass derived from it is quarantined and must not be used for inference, calibration, trend claims, or training.

The 300-paper random corpus and the blind shuffle remain valid data infrastructure. They are preserved because they do not themselves encode a judgment about what SAT is.

## Correct sequence

1. **Build the SAT definition from Nathan-authoritative evidence first.**
   - direct Nathan statements;
   - corrections and counterexamples;
   - "this is SAT / this is not SAT" judgments;
   - "this is an SAT move" judgments;
   - distinctions Nathan says matter structurally, philosophically, or foundationally;
   - historical changes in meaning and current supersession status.

2. **Separate source roles.**
   - Nathan-direct = authority on SAT identity;
   - assistant/LLM text = context, proposal, reconstruction, or hypothesis only;
   - external literature = comparison/control only;
   - archive frequency = evidence about recurrence, not authority about meaning.

3. **Construct candidate tags from the Nathan-authoritative corpus.**
   Every proposed signature tag must be traceable to Nathan evidence and remain provisional until Nathan accepts, rejects, narrows, broadens, or renames it.

4. **Calibrate by examples and counterexamples.**
   Present Nathan with representative cases and record his judgment and reason. Prefer contrast pairs:
   - SAT / not SAT;
   - superficially similar / genuinely SAT-like;
   - same vocabulary / different move;
   - different vocabulary / same SAT move.

5. **Freeze an operational rubric only after Nathan approval.**
   The rubric should encode his criteria in language sufficiently explicit that different LLM instances reproduce the same classification.

6. **Only then score the blinded external corpus.**
   The blind design controls year/author/category bias; it does not substitute model judgment for Nathan's definition.

7. **Unblind only after classifications are frozen.**
   Then compare 2024/2025/2026 prevalence, with all conclusions limited to the approved rubric and the visible abstract evidence.

## What the external corpus can tell us

Once calibrated, the random arXiv corpus can measure how often Nathan-defined SAT moves or structural patterns appear in mainstream physics abstracts, how those patterns co-occur, and whether their prevalence changes across the sampled years.

It cannot determine what SAT is. It cannot promote a model-invented resemblance into an SAT feature. It cannot turn frequency or rarity into definitional authority.

## Continuity / lockdown requirement

Before any SATity judgment task, the working instance must load the current Nathan-authoritative definition/rubric and its examples/counterexamples. If that authority surface is missing, incomplete, or internally unresolved, the task stops at retrieval/description and does not proceed to SAT classification.
