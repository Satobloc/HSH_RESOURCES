In the SAT framework, the **proton-to-electron mass ratio** (\\(\frac{m_p}{m_e}\\)) is determined by comparing their respective topological charges (\\(Q\\)), torsion factors (\\(\tau_\chi\\)), and induced inertial masses (\\(m_{\text{ind}}\\)).

### 1. Electron Mass Calculation
* **Topological Charge (\\(Q_e\\))**: The electron ground state carries \\(Q_e = 1\\).
* **Torsion Factor (\\(\tau_\chi\\))**: Set to \\(\tau_\chi = 0\\), meaning the exponential torsion factor is \\(e^{\gamma_\chi \cdot 0} = 1\\).
* **Induced Mass (\\(m_{\text{ind}}\\))**: \\(m_{\text{ind}, e} \approx 0\text{ MeV}\\).
* **Effective Mass**:
  \\[m_e = \frac{m_0}{Q_e} e^{\gamma_\chi \cdot 0} + m_{\text{ind}} = \frac{0.511\text{ MeV}}{1} \times 1 + 0 \approx \mathbf{0.511\text{ MeV}} \quad\\]

---

### 2. Proton Mass Calculation
* **Topological Charge (\\(Q_p\\))**: The proton (\\(uud\\)) carries \\(Q_p = 3\\).
* **Topological Mass (\\(m_{\text{topo}}\\))**: With base scale \\(m_0 = 938\text{ MeV}\\), the base topological mass per unit charge is:
  \\[m_{\text{topo}, p} = \frac{938\text{ MeV}}{3} \approx 312.7\text{ MeV} \quad\\]
* **Torsion Factor (\\(\tau_\chi\\))**: Set to \\(\tau_\chi = 1\\) with fitted exponent \\(\gamma_\chi \approx 1.068\\), giving an exponential factor \\(e^{\gamma_\chi \tau_\chi} \approx 2.908\\).
* **Induced Mass (\\(m_{\text{ind}}\\))**: Strong gluon binding contributes \\(m_{\text{ind}, p} = 10\text{ MeV}\\).
* **Effective Mass**:
  \\[m_p = (312.7\text{ MeV} + 10\text{ MeV}) \times 2.908 \approx \mathbf{938.27\text{ MeV}} \quad\\]

---

### 3. Proton-Electron Mass Ratio

Dividing the SAT effective proton mass by the electron mass gives:

\\[\frac{m_p}{m_e} = \frac{938.27\text{ MeV}}{0.511\text{ MeV}} \approx \mathbf{1836.15} \quad\\]

*(Inversely, the electron-to-proton mass ratio \\(\frac{m_e}{m_p} \approx \mathbf{0.0005446}\\).)*

In SAT, this large ratio arises because the proton's \\(Q=3\\) world-line topology is exponentially amplified by strong-sector torsion (\\(e^{1.068} \approx 2.908\\)), whereas the electron has a unit topological charge \\(Q=1\\) and zero world-line torsion (\\(\tau_\chi = 0\\)).
