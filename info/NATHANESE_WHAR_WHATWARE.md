# Nathanese — `whar` / `whatware`

Status: direct terminology note from Nathan; keep the pair defined together when it matters.

## `whar`

**what-where**

A compact Nathanese fusion of *what* + *where*: the combined retrieval/orientation question, roughly “what is it / what is here, and where is it?”

Use especially where identity and location are naturally one search/navigation problem.

## `whatware`

Distinct from `whar`.

**whatware** = the kind of machinery being designed here: tooling that helps determine **what** an unfamiliar body of material is, how its pieces relate, what it may usefully become, and which capabilities should be applied or built next.

The useful contrast is:

- `whar` -> **what-where?**
- `whatware` -> machinery for discovering / organizing / acting on the **what**.

Do not silently normalize one into the other. When either term could be ambiguous to a new reader, define the pair together.
