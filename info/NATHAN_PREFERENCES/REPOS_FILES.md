# Nathan Preference Category ⌂ — REPOS / FILES

Status: active source-handling and governed-workspace preferences.

`F01 | WHEN working in any repository | FIRST read the relevant welcome/orientation/guidance/tools/control documents and revisit them when the task/context materially changes.`

`F02 | WHEN a document upload/file is instantiated for the task | Review the whole relevant document before assuming memory, summaries, filenames, snippets, or earlier familiarity suffice for a judgment that depends on the whole.`

`F03 | WHEN a source is directly available | Prefer direct inspection over memory-derived reconstruction.`

`F04 | ALWAYS | Never infer substantive file contents from filename/path alone when claim-level evidence is required.`

`F05 | WHEN a repo has quarantine/sandbox boundaries | Determine and obey them before crossing, copying, synthesizing, or routing material; preserve exposure state.`

`F06 | WHEN working in PRIOR_ART or another quarantined environment | Treat its controls as hard; do not leak content/reasoning into ordinary surfaces except through explicitly cleared interfaces.`

`F07 | WHEN a standing all-worker/project rule exists | Treat it as applicable within its stated scope; do not silently substitute remembered older controls.`

`F08 | WHEN reading indexes/summaries/wayfinding | Treat them as maps unless the repo explicitly makes them authority; follow through to primary/controlling material for substantive judgments.`

`F09 | WHEN provenance matters | Preserve exact path/source/date/authorship/currentness/coverage status and distinguish original from generated summary or later interpretation.`

`F10 | WHEN source coverage is partial | State the coverage honestly; do not silently upgrade skim/snippet/targeted-read familiarity to full review.`

`F11 | WHEN writing shared/hot control surfaces | Prefer a minimal bounded semantic change over opportunistic cleanup unless the task explicitly calls for broader revision.`

`F12 | WHEN modifying a repo | Respect its write conventions, preserve originals, and make reversible/local changes where practical before broad restructuring.`

`F13 | HSH_RESOURCES SPECIAL | Treat HSH_RESOURCES as supporting-resource/reference infrastructure, not SAT/H(s)H theory authority. Inclusion or usefulness there does not promote a theory claim.`

`F14 | WHEN current source is available but memory conflicts | Inspect current source and privilege the inspected governing source for the task; surface meaningful conflict rather than silently merging.`

`F15 | WHEN a repository task can be completed in one turn | Do a reasonable bounded amount of actual work with a reasonable number of calls; do not nibble around edges or launch an unbounded refactor.`

`F16 | WHEN creating continuity/handoff state | Record enough exact state that another instance can resume without redoing already completed source work.`
