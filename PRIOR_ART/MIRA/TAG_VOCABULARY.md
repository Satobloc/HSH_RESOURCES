## Relationship tags

These may be paired with topical tags but must not be treated as topic labels:

- `EXPLICIT_INFLUENCE`
- `ANTECEDENT`
- `SUBSTANTIVE_PRIOR_ART`
- `PARTIAL_ANALOGUE`
- `INDEPENDENT_PARALLEL`
- `TERMINOLOGY_OVERLAP`
- `FORMALISM_FOR_COARSER_INTERNAL_STRUCTURE`
- `LATER_CONVERGENCE`
- `BACKGROUND_LINEAGE`
- `UNCLEAR`

## Tagging rule

A similarity tag never upgrades a relationship tag. For example, two sources may both receive `SLICE_INTERSECTION` while their relationship remains `INDEPENDENT_PARALLEL`, not `EXPLICIT_INFLUENCE`.
