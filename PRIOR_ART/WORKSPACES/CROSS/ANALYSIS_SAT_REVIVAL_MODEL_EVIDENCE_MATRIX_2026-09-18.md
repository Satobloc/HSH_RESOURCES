## 1. Purpose

The Cross investigation began with a broad question about possible outward diffusion from SAT/H(s)H into later scientific and quasi-scientific discourse. Earlier discussion often compressed that problem into a remembered working credence of roughly 91% for diffuse percolation / field-dialogue shaping. The separate Analysis audit has already reconstructed and pruned that older case in `ANALYSIS_PRE_CROSS_ARGUMENT_AUDIT_2026-09-18.md`.

The present report sharpens the target.

The most salient question is no longer primarily:

> Did SAT directly cause particular physicists to publish particular SAT-like ideas?

The better question is:

> **Did SAT materially increase the salience, circulation, redevelopment, recombination, or accessibility of relatively obscure branches of Minkowski-descended / higher-dimensional / geometric physics that already existed?**

This is a **revival / popularization hypothesis**, not principally a direct-copying hypothesis.

That distinction matters because a revival can occur through several mechanisms that do not require proposition-level borrowing:

- old ideas become newly noticeable;
- adjacent theorists become motivated to finish, formalize, distinguish, or publish their own independent work;
- obscure specialist material becomes easier to surface through search, AI, repositories, and cross-domain discussion;
- different independent projects reinforce one another's visibility;
- ideas migrate indirectly through small social or machine-mediated networks;
- professional work may be accelerated, recombined, or reframed without direct attribution to SAT.

The current project therefore treats **salience transfer**, **developmental activation**, and **idea transfer** as distinct causal possibilities.

# 2. Authority and evidentiary rules

## 2.1 Nathan alone defines SAT/H(s)H

Only Nathan may state what SAT/H(s)H is or is not.

The working models may reconstruct a candidate structural fingerprint from the historical corpus, but such a reconstruction is not itself authoritative theory definition. For any proposition, mechanism, solver, term, or interpretation, Analysis must distinguish at least:

- **Nathan-confirmed current interpretation**;
- **historically recorded Nathan statement at date X**;
- **model reconstruction / inference**;
- **external comparison**.

An external resemblance cannot retroactively define SAT/H(s)H.

## 2.2 Intent testimony and historical record answer different questions

Nathan's present testimony is authoritative for his intentions, meanings, and whether a present reconstruction matches what he means by SAT/H(s)H.

But historical claims such as:

- what had been developed by a given date;
- what was publicly stated;
- what was provisional or rejected;
- what an outside listener could have encountered;

must be established from the historical record where available.

The practical hierarchy is:

1. contemporaneous primary record — dated conversations, notebooks, drafts, code, images, commits, recordings, etc.;
2. podcast transcripts / episode records — currently the strongest record of what was publicly available at a given podcast date;
3. repository history and other publication records — especially file-by-file / commit-by-commit where exact content matters;
4. Nathan's retrospective testimony — authoritative for intent and interpretation but distinguishable from contemporaneous recording;
5. model reconstruction and summaries — navigation and hypothesis generation only.

A core absence rule follows:

> **Not yet found in the currently uploaded and ingested corpus does not mean not present historically.**

Only a small fraction of the full historical record is presently uploaded, and a smaller fraction has been fully ingested by current working models.

## 2.3 Two clocks must remain separate

For revival/diffusion work, at least two historical clocks matter:

- **internal-development clock:** when Nathan had a proposition or structure privately;
- **public-exposure clock:** when outsiders could actually encounter it.

Where possible, each relevant SAT/H(s)H feature should eventually receive three no-later-than dates:

- earliest private-record NLT;
- earliest public-podcast NLT;
- earliest public-archive/repository NLT.

## 2.4 Current rejection does not erase historical diffusion eligibility

A proposition can be rejected or superseded in current SAT/H(s)H and remain fully relevant to historical revival/diffusion analysis.

For example, a 24-cell lattice proposition, if publicly articulated during an exposure window, remains eligible as a possible historical fingerprint even if Nathan now rejects it.

Conversely, present adoption does not make a feature publicly available earlier than the record shows.

For every proposition used in outward-diffusion analysis, keep separate:

- historical SAT/H(s)H status at date X;
- current Nathan status;
- public availability date;
- diffusion eligibility at the relevant comparison date;
- external comparison status.

This prevents survivor bias toward only present-day theory content.

# 3. Central hypothesis and subordinate causal effects

## 3.1 Primary hypothesis R — SAT revival / popularization

> **After becoming public, SAT contributed to increased salience, circulation, redevelopment, or recombination of relatively obscure Minkowski-descended / higher-dimensional / geometric-physics ideas. This effect need not involve copying SAT, adoption of SAT terminology, or even direct awareness of SAT by later authors.**

The target variable is therefore **salience/activity in an idea ecology**, not merely ancestry.

## 3.2 Salience transfer

SAT may make an old or obscure direction newly noticeable, discussable, searchable, or worth revisiting.

Examples of the hypothesized subjective response:

- “Would you look at that.”
- “Someone else is working in this territory.”
- “I had not thought about this branch in years.”
- “There may be an audience for this kind of model.”

No proposition-level borrowing is required.

## 3.3 Developmental activation

An adjacent theorist may already possess an independently developed model, encounter SAT, and then devote more effort to finishing, formalizing, differentiating, publishing, or extending that model.

Nathan supplies a direct mechanism example in the opposite direction: by his testimony, seeing QFiT gave him a “kick in the butt” to develop SAT further. This does **not** establish SAT→other-theorist activation, but it demonstrates that adjacent-theory exposure can causally accelerate development without supplying the core ideas.

This mechanism is particularly important because it makes “independent development” and “SAT had causal impact” logically compatible.

## 3.4 Idea transfer

The stronger mechanism is actual transfer of SAT propositions, architectures, terminology, derivations, or conceptual packages into another work or discourse.

This remains possible but is **not required** for the primary revival hypothesis.

# 4. Deep historical substrate: the 4D / Minkowski / geometric tradition

Prior-art work now shows that the relevant intellectual soil is much older than SAT and older than Minkowski spacetime itself.

The current quarantined prior-art report identifies two especially strong early comparators:

- **Charles Howard Hinton**, particularly *The Fourth Dimension* (1904), with a moving-film construction in which a spiral intersecting a lower-dimensional film yields apparent circular motion, extended to structures consisting of filaments whose intersections appear as atoms and bodies;
- **W. Whately Smith** (1920), with threads piercing a lower-dimensional film, apparent atoms as intersections, large twisted/intertwined filamentary structures, and extension to N-space.

See `2026-09-14_CROSS_PRELIMINARY_PRIOR_ART_REPORT.md`.

These are meaningful prior-art hits for broad section/intersection architecture. They are not evidence that SAT derived from Hinton or Smith.

The same prior-art report also finds a modern self-aware professional lineage in nLab that explicitly connects:

> Klein / Poincaré / Minkowski → Cartan geometry → first-order gravity → super-Cartan geometry / supergravity → higher Cartan geometry / higher gauge theory / branes.

This is important for two reasons that point in opposite directions.

### 4.1 Prior art weakens naive novelty signals

The existence of worldlines, higher-dimensional sections, geometric gravity, topology, filaments, intersections, and related structures cannot by itself serve as strong evidence of SAT influence. Many such ingredients are old.

A valid revival analysis must distinguish:

- ingredient prior art;
- functional prior art — same ingredient doing the same job;
- package prior art — same cluster of functions together;
- architectural / dependency-graph prior art — same causal or derivational ordering;
- historical salience — how active/visible the branch was before SAT;
- ordinary lineage availability — whether an outside author already had a natural route to the same material.

### 4.2 Prior art can increase the plausibility of recognition and relay

The revival hypothesis requires something to revive.

If small, specialized communities already carried these older traditions, then SAT need not teach them the basic mathematics. A new independently developed variant may instead be **recognized** as occupying familiar territory.

The key hypothesis is therefore not “specialists had forgotten Minkowski-like geometry.” The more careful proposition is:

> Some branches may have remained alive inside very small specialist networks while being low-salience in broader theoretical-physics and public discourse.

Nathan's hypothesis that such communities are tiny and socially tight is plausible but not yet empirically established for each relevant branch. Citation/coauthor/conference-network work should eventually test it.

A useful narrower hypothesis is:

> **Hμ — one or a few technically engaged listeners in or adjacent to a small relevant community encountered SAT and relayed it informally.**

This is much weaker than claiming that physicists broadly discovered SAT.

This mechanism predicts:

- bursty rather than smooth secondary acquisition;
- private rather than public citation trails;

Nathan states that encountering QFiT gave him a “kick in the butt” to develop SAT further.

This is valuable analytically because it demonstrates a mechanism that does not fit a simple binary of “copied” versus “independent.”

The reciprocal possibility is therefore legitimate to test:

> encounter SAT → recognize adjacency / competition / validation → increase effort on pre-existing independent theory.

One observed Nathan-side case does not prove the reciprocal population effect, but it makes **developmental activation** a concrete causal category rather than a speculative placeholder.

# 12. External-science side: what must actually change for revival to be demonstrated

The original impact discussion collected many later papers/news items that appeared SAT-adjacent. Prior-art work has already shown why raw similarity counting is not enough.

The mature unit of comparison is **functional architecture / dependency structure**, not vocabulary.

Generic ingredients such as:

- worldlines;
- topology;
- holonomy;
- dark photons;
- geometric gravity;
- compactification;
- line defects;
- parameter-free rhetoric;

are weak evidence because they have substantial independent histories.

The revival hypothesis therefore requires evidence that the **salience or joint arrangement** of relevant obscure structures changed after SAT exposure beyond what older lineages and contemporary common causes predict.

The main empirical question is:

> Did the prevalence, visibility, or recombination of relevant low-salience branches increase after SAT's public/exposure boundaries, and was the increase disproportionately concentrated in structures or packages that SAT emphasized?

This must be measured with pre/post baselines and denominators rather than anecdotal chronology.

# 15. Null / alternative causal models

“Independent convergence” is too coarse to serve as one omnibus null. The following alternatives should be tested separately.

## N0 — no meaningful revival

The apparent cluster is largely selection bias, retrospective pattern matching, publication sampling, or denominator neglect.

Prediction: a denominator-complete pre/post analysis makes most of the apparent shift disappear.

## N1 — endogenous specialist revival

The relevant branches were already becoming more active for reasons internal to mathematics/physics.

Prediction: the upward trajectory begins before SAT's public boundary and follows ordinary citation/program lineage.

## N2 — broad contemporary pressures

AI-for-science, DESI, quantum information, topology, emergent spacetime, new experiments, and adjacent developments simultaneously pushed many groups toward similar structures.

Prediction: matched neighboring fields and control concepts rise similarly, with no special concentration around SAT-emphasized packages.

## N3 — broader speculative/AI ecology without SAT being important

SAT, QFiT, and other adjacent projects are parallel products of the same cultural/technical environment.

Prediction: many independent filament/geometric theories appear around the same time without plausible SAT-facing routes, and their structure follows shared AI/literature priors.

## N4 — QFiT or other adjacent projects were the important catalysts

SAT participated in the ecology but was not a major driver.

Prediction: salience changes align better with QFiT/other visibility than with SAT exposure boundaries.

## R — SAT materially contributed

SAT need not be the sole or dominant driver.

A defensible eventual conclusion could be:

> SAT was one identifiable catalyst in a broader 2025–26 revival of an obscure geometric-physics ecology.

That is materially weaker than “SAT caused the revival” and much stronger than “SAT merely happened to resemble later work.”

# 16. Predictions of the revival model

If R is substantially true, several patterns should be more likely.

## 16.1 Low-prevalence combinations should matter more than generic words

The largest post-exposure changes should occur in relatively uncommon **combinations / functions / dependency structures**, not merely generic “topology,” “worldline,” or “geometry.”

## 16.2 Lag structure should align with exposure depth

Possible stages:

- Feb 2025 podcast public boundary;
- May 2025 QFiT public appearance;
- Oct 2025 SAT repository public availability;
- Dec 2025 archive-publicization milestone;
- 2026 larger acquisition/search/discourse effects.

A revival signal should have chronologically plausible delays relative to these surfaces.

## 16.3 Adjacent theorists may accelerate without importing SAT vocabulary

Developmental activation predicts some later works will look like natural continuations of pre-existing private/public programs but appear earlier, more developed, or more visible than expected.

This is difficult to prove but conceptually important: absence of SAT terminology does not negate a catalyst effect.

## 16.4 Search interest should move in baskets

If SAT participates in a salience shock, matched obscure SAT-adjacent terms may rise more than matched obscure controls, subject to Trends normalization constraints.

## 16.5 Mixed ancestry should be common

Later work may combine:

- old Minkowski/Cartan/higher-geometry lineage;
- contemporary mainstream pressures;
- QFiT/open-filament ecology;
- SAT-mediated salience;
- AI recombination;
- independent innovation.

A revival model therefore expects messy, indirect ancestry rather than clean one-source copying.

# 17. What would strongly weaken the revival hypothesis

The model should be downgraded if prior-art and baseline work shows that:

- the supposedly obscure branches were already undergoing a clear revival before Feb 2025;
- post-2025 activity is no greater than matched historical variation;
- candidate external works were already substantially complete or publicly circulating before SAT exposure;
- their citation lineages naturally and compactly explain the same architectural packages;
- matched unrelated obscure controls exhibit the same apparent search/publication rise;
- SAT-facing exposure windows fail to align with any measurable salience change;
- repository clone activity resolves overwhelmingly into irrelevant automated services with no plausible semantic use;
- QFiT and other apparent relay nodes have independent developmental records that fully explain the later ecology without any SAT-associated contribution.

The key standard is not whether every row can be independently explained. One or two broad common causes that compactly explain the full cluster are sufficient to preserve the null.
