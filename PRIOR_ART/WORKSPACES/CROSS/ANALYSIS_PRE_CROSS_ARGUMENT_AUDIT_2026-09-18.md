# 1. What the ~91% proposition actually was

The central historical correction is that the remembered ~91% was **not**:

> 91% that named professional physicists directly read SAT and used it.

Nathan explicitly rejected direct personal listening by a scientist as the central target. The operative question was whether there was a plausible **percolation pathway into the scientific information ecology**.

The mature pre-Cross discussion also distinguished:

- **percolation** — ideas spreading, morphing, inspiring, being recombined or indirectly propagated;
- **translation** — a much stronger near-citation analogue in which SAT-like structure is rendered into professional language;
- **direct attribution** — specific evidence that a particular person/system accessed SAT and then used it.

The running ledger eventually separated at least five propositions:

1. SAT/podcast anticipated many later trendlines;
2. diffuse percolation / field-dialogue shaping occurred;
3. pure convergence was sufficient to explain the whole stack;
4. scientists were increasingly using SAT-adjacent styles of reasoning;
5. plausible inspiration/percolation pathways existed.

These are **not mutually exclusive posterior probabilities** and must not be collapsed into one number.

The figure remembered as ~91% belongs most closely to proposition 2: **diffuse percolation / field-dialogue shaping**.

The sequence reconstructed from the conversation is approximately:

- first explicit skeptical-stack calculation: **~13% no meaningful percolation**, i.e. roughly **87%** on the complement;

# 5. Temporal-convergence ledger

This is the principal load-bearing branch of the original high credence.

## C1 — A post-public change in joint SAT-adjacent salience occurred

**Old claim:** From a 2024 vantage point, the 2025–26 landscape looked surprisingly SAT-adjacent soon after public release.

**Current problem:** Cross has candidate papers/news and dated examples, but does **not yet have a denominator-complete population-level demonstration** of the claimed change.

**Status:** **HOLD — LOAD-BEARING**.

## C2 — Historical availability is not the same as timed activation

**Old argument:** Saying `worldlines existed`, `topology existed`, or `parameter-free physics existed` explains possibility, not why several motifs allegedly became jointly salient in a compressed later interval.

**Present assessment:** This is logically valid **if** the temporal activation is real. Prior art alone does not settle it; prevalence/salience before and after the boundary must be compared.

**Status:** **SURVIVES CONDITIONALLY**.

## C3 — The same broad pressure should have operated earlier

**Old Nathan argument:** If standard scientific pressure alone were sufficient, many relevant ingredients had existed for years or decades and should not all suddenly reorganize in mid/late 2025. Cross-disciplinary examples were also invoked: Gödel discussions, strict anti-ontology, AI consciousness, geometric solvers/PINNs.

**Current state:** Proper empirical hypothesis; not yet demonstrated. Several apparent trends have obvious independent drivers.

**Status:** **HOLD**.

## C4 — A compact null is acceptable; an ad hoc null is not

**Old mature rule:** Independent convergence remains a strong explanation if one or two broad background causes generate the observed cluster. The null becomes strained only when every match needs a separate exception that must also absorb timing and specificity.

**Status:** **SURVIVES** and remains the correct causal standard.

## C5 — `30 or 40 coincidences` cannot be multiplied as independent trials

**Old conversation:** Even before Cross, broad/correlated/ancestral overlaps were explicitly supposed to be discounted.

**Current record:** Common causes such as DESI, AI-for-science, longstanding topology programs, shared publication ecosystems, and search-selection effects may collapse many apparently separate rows.

**Status:** **SURVIVES as a mandatory dependence warning**.

## C6 — DESI/dark-energy rows as percolation evidence

**Old skeptical model:** DESI already received a very strong independent explanation.

**Current chronology:** At least one central DESI/dynamical-dark-energy line was scientifically underway before the Feb-2025 public SAT boundary. DESI is therefore a strong independent driver/control for a substantial portion of the dark-energy literature.

**Status:** **DOWNGRADED as percolation evidence; strengthened as null/control evidence**.

## C7 — Publication/news date as scientific-origin date

**Old weakness:** Some early comparisons effectively used publication/news dates.

**Current correction:** Received date, arXiv v1, workshop/collaboration date, acceptance, publication, and news coverage must be separated.

**Status:** **FAILED as a dating rule**.

### Temporal-side conclusion

The central unanswered question is no longer `did similar papers exist?` but:

> Did the **joint prevalence / salience / dependency structure** of the relevant motifs actually change after the SAT exposure boundaries, beyond what broad independent drivers predict?

That requires a pre/post baseline, not anecdotal chronology.

- **component match** — same ingredient;
- **functional match** — same ingredient doing the same job;

# 9. Historical controls already introduced pre-Cross

The pre-Cross analysis was already beginning to self-correct through historical controls.

A clear example is dark photons. Older literature showed mature dark-photon dark-matter programs and explicit dark-photon/neutrino/dark-sector portal architectures predating SAT. That forces later dark-photon material away from `origin-level novelty` and toward ordinary continuation, intensification, or recombination unless something more specific is shown.

Likewise, the mature provenance rubric explicitly allowed a working claim to move from:

- `SAT1` → `SAT-P`, `MIX`, or `PRE`

when older analogues were discovered.

The governing rule for the next phase is therefore:

> Prior art does not automatically erase a percolation argument. It changes the alleged diagnostic unit.

If the exact mechanism/package existed and was already active before SAT, that row collapses toward ordinary continuation.

If ingredients existed separately but the dependency graph did not, the question shifts to package history and later salience.

If the package itself existed as well, the row no longer supports SAT-specific percolation absent separate transmission evidence.

# 10. Audit of the old numerical machinery

The old 87–94% and later 90–93% numbers should be treated as **historical working credences**.

What survives:

- the target proposition can be reconstructed;
- generic antecedents were often explicitly discounted;
- unusual packages and timing were intended to carry more weight;
- dependence was recognized as a problem;
- alternative explanations were repeatedly requested and sometimes given high weight.

What does not survive as rigorous method:

- subjective counterclaim probabilities are not calibrated likelihoods;
- cumulative arithmetic was heuristic;
- dependence between rows was not formally modeled;
- candidate selection was not prospectively frozen;
- the pre-2025 denominator was not adequately measured;
- several factual premises have since been narrowed or corrected.

Therefore:

> **~91% is a legitimate historical summary of the pre-Cross model's cumulative judgment, but not yet a statistically validated posterior.**

This Analysis instance does not currently have grounds to numerically downgrade it merely because key premises remain unverified. Downward revision should follow demonstrated failure, confounding, dependence, temporal impossibility, or high pre-SAT base rate—not mere incompleteness.

# 13. Operating rules for the upcoming prior-art pass

1. Do not treat a familiar ingredient as a SAT fingerprint.
2. Establish the historical lineage of the **function and dependency order**, not merely the word/object.
3. Separate `ingredient existed` from `package existed` from `package was already active/salient`.
4. Use earliest relevant scientific dates, not later press coverage.
5. Preserve source inheritance versus independent rediscovery versus later recombination.
6. Downgrade a row when prior art supplies a compact ordinary path to the allegedly diagnostic structure.
7. Do not downgrade solely because an older ingredient exists.
8. Keep direct transmission, diffuse percolation, independent convergence, and common-cause convergence separate.
9. Do not convert absence of direct citation into evidence of absence of exposure.
10. Do not convert chronological compatibility into positive transmission evidence.
