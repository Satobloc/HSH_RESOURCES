# Schreiber / Hypothesis H / Minkowski-Lineage Research Plan

**Status:** ACTIVE / QUARANTINED  
**Owner:** Alden Cross  
**Created:** 2026-09-15 EDT

## Purpose

Build a source-first historical and bibliographic record of Urs Schreiber's relevant work without assuming in advance which items do or do not belong to Hypothesis H. The resulting record will support later priority acknowledgments, comparison with SAT/H(s)H, citation of Schreiber/Sati discussions of Hypothesis H development, and a relationship map of relevant Minkowski-descended geometric research programs.

This work is historical/provenance analysis, not forward H(s)H theory development. Substantive prior-art notes remain quarantine-bound until deliberately released with attribution.

## Core methodological rule

Do **not** search only for "Hypothesis H" and then reconstruct its past from the present label.

First reconstruct Schreiber's dated research activity as neutrally as possible. Only after the chronology exists should items be tagged as:

- explicit Hypothesis H;
- retrospectively identified by Schreiber/Sati as part of the HH program;
- clear antecedent/background used by HH;
- adjacent/convergent work;
- downstream application/consequence;
- unrelated/uncertain.

The classification itself must cite the evidence that justifies it.

## Deliverable A — Schreiber chronology ledger

One row/event per dated item where possible:

`date | item | type | author(s) | venue/surface | topic in source's own terms | cited antecedents | later HH classification? | evidence for classification | source`

Candidate item types include:
- papers/preprints;
- talks/slides/lecture notes;
- nLab/nForum/Schreiber-wiki page creation and substantial revisions;
- research-program pages;
- comments/discussion posts;
- bibliographic additions that reveal retrospective genealogy;
- project descriptions, CV/research-page statements, conference abstracts;
- explicit statements about origins, development, open problems, or research-program boundaries.

When revision histories are available, preserve first appearance separately from later rewriting.

## Deliverable B — HH development / self-description sourcebook

Collect directly attributable Schreiber and Sati statements about:

- when and how Hypothesis H arose;
- what the name "Hypothesis H" denotes at different dates;
- what mathematical/physical problem the program is intended to address;
- which earlier results they identify as motivating or antecedent;
- which papers/results they treat as foundational, tests, implications, or applications;
- what remains conjectural/open;
- how they describe relations among super-Cartan geometry, higher gauge theory, cohomotopy, branes, configuration spaces, braid/anyon structures, and related machinery;
- how they describe any historical lineage from Minkowski/Klein/Cartan onward.

Preserve dates and wording context. Do not turn later retrospective summaries into evidence that a 2010-era item was already conceived as "HH" unless the source establishes that.

## Deliverable C — braid / configuration-space relationship ledger

Track Schreiber/Sati material concerning:

- braid groups and braid categories;
- configuration spaces;
- worldline/braid descriptions;
- anyons;
- M5-brane applications;
- higher braid groups / homotopy-theoretic generalizations where explicitly present;
- statements connecting these structures to Hypothesis H, brane physics, or earlier work.

Keep three relations distinct:
1. ordinary mathematical background used in the program;
2. a formal consequence/application of HH;
3. a historical influence or developmental input explicitly claimed by the authors.

Do not infer (3) from (1) or (2).

## Deliverable D — priority/citation register for later SAT/H(s)H comparison

Do not decide SAT/H(s)H novelty globally. Instead build a granular register:

`structure/claim | earliest external source found | author(s) | date | exact result/idea | later relevant source(s) | SAT/H(s)H earliest independent record | relationship status | citation-ready reference`

Possible relationship statuses:
- clear external priority on same construction;
- external priority on mathematical tool/background only;
- related but materially different construction;
- possible independent convergence;
- chronological order known but conceptual relation unresolved;
- no priority conclusion yet.

The register is intended to make later acknowledgments precise rather than sweeping.

## Deliverable E — Minkowski-lineage relationship map

Construct a bibliographic/historical graph rather than a single linear family tree.

### Node classes
- people;
- named research programs/theories;
- mathematical frameworks;
- landmark papers/books;
- institutions/discussion communities when historically relevant.

### Edge types
Use explicit typed edges, preferably with source/date:
- `cites`;
- `explicitly develops/generalizes`;
- `uses mathematical framework of`;
- `coauthored-with`;
- `retrospectively identifies as antecedent`;
- `historically influenced by` (only with source support);
- `formal specialization/reduction/generalization` (only when mathematically established);
- `structural analogy` (Cross/SAT comparison, clearly marked as analyst-created rather than historical fact);
- `shared ancestor / common lineage`.

Avoid unlabeled arrows implying descent.

### Initial historical backbone to test, not assume
Potential nodes/edges to investigate include:
- Minkowski / spacetime geometry;
- Klein / Erlangen geometry;
- Cartan / Cartan geometry and torsion;
- relativity / first-order gravity lineages;
- higher category/homotopy/higher-gauge developments;
- string/worldsheet and p-brane programs;
- braid/configuration-space traditions;
- Sati-Schreiber super-Cartan / higher gauge / cohomotopy program;
- Hypothesis H;
- SAT/H(s)H as a separately documented independent-development node, linked only after chronology and structural comparison are established.

This is a graph of documented intellectual/formal relationships, not a claim that all nodes constitute one continuous school.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer, in order:
1. dated original papers/preprints and books;
2. dated talks/slides/lecture notes by the relevant authors;
3. Schreiber/Sati research pages and nLab/Schreiber-wiki pages with revision history;
4. nForum or other attributable discussion/comments;
5. contemporaneous third-party citations/descriptions;
6. later retrospective summaries, clearly labeled retrospective.

For nLab, distinguish:
- current page content;
- first creation date;
- revision history;
- named contributor where available;
- cited source date.

A current nLab page is not automatically evidence that its present framing existed when an older cited paper was written.

## Preliminary public anchors already verified at plan creation

These are navigation anchors only, not a completed genealogy:

- nLab states that the site was created in November 2008 by Urs Schreiber.
- The current nLab Hypothesis H page states that the core idea originates with an observation due to Hisham Sati (2013, sec. 2.5) and became a research program developed by Sati and Schreiber.
- A Schreiber talk titled *Some Quantum States of M-Branes under Hypothesis H* is dated 9 Dec 2021 and explicitly describes joint work with Hisham Sati.
- Current Schreiber research pages list Hypothesis H and geometric engineering of anyons on M5-branes as distinct named projects; their precise historical relation must therefore be reconstructed rather than assumed.
- Current HH introductory materials include a section on double dimensional reduction and a separate anyon/M5-brane component, but present organization must not be projected backward without dated evidence.

## First-pass workflow

1. Inventory Schreiber-authored/coauthored papers, talks, research-wiki pages, and relevant nLab/nForum material by date.
2. Capture revision histories where feasible.
3. Build the chronology ledger before assigning HH-status tags.
4. Extract explicit self-historical statements into the HH sourcebook.
5. Build braid/configuration-space ledger from direct sources.
6. Populate granular priority register.
7. Build the typed Minkowski-lineage graph from bibliographic evidence.
8. Only afterward compare SAT/H(s)H structures against the resulting external map.

## Quarantine / release rule

No substantive result from this plan enters ordinary H(s)H theory-building surfaces by silent paraphrase. Releaseable outputs should use original bibliographic references and explicit attribution. Internal retrieval paths/hashes may remain private.
