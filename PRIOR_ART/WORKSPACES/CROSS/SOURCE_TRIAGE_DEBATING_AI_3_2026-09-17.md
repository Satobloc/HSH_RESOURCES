## Additional direct Nathan material located in this pass

### Physics `proof` language
Nathan explicitly says that physics does not provide proof in the mathematical sense; it provides evidence and tests consistency with observations. Later, when asked how to `empirically prove` SAT claims, he again says `proof` is not a thing and that the task is to obtain numbers and compare them with observations.

### 24-cell / lattice correction
Nathan says the 24-cell lattice is not the active SAT formalism. He describes it as something briefly explored and potentially usable as a coordinate/grid representation, while stating that the current work proceeds with isolated worldline geometry. Later questions that describe SAT as fundamentally a 24-cell-lattice theory are directly corrected.

### LLM audit methodology
Nathan rejects the description of LLM audits as `neutral`. He says LLMs are susceptible both to agreement-seeking and to confident negative answers when prompted to criticize. He describes using multiple passes, opposed roles, personal interrogation, and a current workflow in which NotebookLM performs calculations that are audited by ChatGPT and himself. He characterizes this methodology as trial-and-error rather than a finished theory of `LLM ecological design`.

### Podcast purpose and audience catering
Nathan describes the podcast primarily as a vehicle for working through his own thoughts, with entertainment secondary. He says increased listener-facing organization/iconography came later, after listeners persisted and the material became difficult to navigate. He denies deliberately filtering early listeners in order to isolate a particular physicist or professional audience.

### Mainstream-awareness / establishment premise
When asked why `mainstream academia universally` views SAT as radical, Nathan rejects the premise and says mainstream academia does not universally view it because only a small number of people have heard of the theory; he gives `upwards of 25 people` as the rough figure in this compiled answer. Record that as Nathan's own statement in this source, not as independently verified audience reach.

In a later question about parameter tuning, Nathan also rejects the premise that he is accusing the scientific community of artificially tuning equations to hide gaps. He describes the Standard Model's data-first procedure as the appropriate response when underlying structure is unknown.

### Ontology / model distinction
Nathan rejects a question premise that treats his geometry as literal truth and other models as mere tricks. He says there is no accessible `literal truth` in that sense and frames the question instead in terms of whether a model/trick works.

### AI contribution / authorship
Nathan says LLMs are an important part of the system, especially for organization, and that they have produced specific innovations at points along the way. He simultaneously says he has been the main driver of most SAT innovation. He states that if he publishes a scientific paper he would credit the LLM contribution, while noting that current scientific authorship conventions would not permit listing them as co-authors.
